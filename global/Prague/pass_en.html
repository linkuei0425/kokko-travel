Q1: Does the Prague 24-hour transport pass include the Petřín Funicular?

Ans: Yes! Prague's 24-hour (App 140 CZK / Paper 150 CZK) and 72-hour long-term public transport passes officially include free rides on the Petřín funicular. Short-term 30-minute or 90-minute tickets do not include it.

Q2: What attractions are included in the Prague Visitor Pass?

Ans: The Prague Visitor Pass covers over 60 popular attractions, including Prague Castle (St. Vitus Cathedral, Old Royal Palace, St. George's Basilica, Golden Lane), Old Town Hall & Astronomical Clock tower, Petřín Lookout Tower, Jewish Museum historical sites, National Museum, and Vltava River Cruise.

Q3: How can I book Prague sightseeing passes online?

Ans: You can click 'View Details' on any ticket in this navigation app and click the official Klook booking button inside the pop-up modal for fast online booking.

Best Value Itinerary & Planning Recommendations

1. Quick Flash (1 Day): If you are staying in Prague for just one day, we recommend buying a 24-hour public transport pass (App 140 CZK). Old Town Square, Charles Bridge, and Malá Strana are easily walkable; use public transit to reach Prague Castle efficiently (Petřín funicular included).

2. In-Depth Sightseeing (2-3 Days): If you plan to visit multiple indoor paid attractions (St. Vitus Cathedral, Old Town Hall Tower, Golden Lane, and museums), purchasing the Prague Visitor Pass (48h / 72h / 120h) seamlessly integrates transit and 60+ attractions, saving you time and hassle.

3. Flexible Exploration: If you prefer strolling streets and picking specific landmarks, buy a 24/72-hour transport pass combined with individual tickets (like Astronomical Clock tower or Castle Circuit B).
