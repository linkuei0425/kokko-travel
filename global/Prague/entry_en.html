Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Visa Regulations: Good news for travelers holding a Republic of China (Taiwan) passport! When visiting the Czech Republic, a proud member of the Schengen Area, you can enjoy visa-free entry for stays up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Just make sure your passport remains valid for at least three months beyond your intended departure date from the Schengen Area.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Customs Prohibited and Restricted Items: As a member of the European Union, the Czech Republic's customs regulations align with standard EU policies. Here's a quick guide to common prohibited and restricted items: 1. **Prohibited Items**: Absolutely no drugs, firearms, ammunition, explosives, endangered plants or animals and their products, pirated or counterfeit goods, or uninspected meat, dairy products, and fresh fruits/vegetables. 2. **Restricted Items**: * **Cash**: If you're carrying more than 10,000 Euros (or its equivalent in other currencies) when entering or leaving the EU, you must declare it to customs. * **Tobacco and Alcohol**: There are quantity limits, such as 200 cigarettes, 4 liters of wine, or 1 liter of spirits (for travelers aged 17 and above). * **Personal Medications**: It's highly recommended to carry a doctor's prescription or a medical certificate for any personal medications, just in case customs officials ask for verification. Always adhere to EU and Czech customs regulations to avoid penalties and ensure a smooth journey!

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration & EES

Paper Immigration Cards and Online Electronic Declaration Systems: For travelers holding a Republic of China (Taiwan) passport: 1. **Paper Immigration Card**: When entering the Czech Republic (Schengen Area), Taiwan passport holders typically do not need to fill out any paper immigration cards. Customs officers will directly stamp your passport upon clearance. 2. **Online Electronic Declaration**: Currently, the Czech Republic does not have a dedicated online electronic entry declaration system for travelers to pre-fill. All entry procedures are handled directly by immigration officers upon arrival at airports or land borders. 3. **EU EES (Entry/Exit System)**: * **No Pre-Registration Required**: Travelers do not need to pre-register or fill out online forms before departure. * **Biometric Data Collection**: Upon arriving at the first Schengen entry point, border officials or self-service kiosks will collect biometric information (fingerprints and facial photos). * **Digital Tracking**: EES replaces traditional manual passport stamps with automated digital records for future entries and exits.

💡 Best Exchange Spots

Top Recommended Exchange Offices and How to Get There: 1. **eXchange**: * **Address**: Politických vězňů 913/4, 110 00 Nové Město * **Directions & Landmarks**: Located right in the city center, just a stone's throw from Wenceslas Square. You can take a tram to Jindřišská station, or Metro Line A/B to Můstek station, followed by a 5-minute walk. Renowned for its fair rates and no hidden fees.

💳 Payment Tips

Currency Exchange Tips: 1. **Avoid Airports and Train Stations**: Avoid exchanging money at airports or train stations. These locations typically offer unfavorable exchange rates and higher fees. 2. **Carefully Compare Exchange Rates**: Before exchanging, always compare the 'Buy' and 'Sell' rates at different offices and confirm there are no hidden fees. 3. **Watch out for '0% Commission'**: Be wary of '0% Commission' signs. Some exchange offices display '0% Commission,' but their exchange rates might be very poor. 4. **Exchange Small Amounts**: It's advisable not to exchange large sums at once to minimize risk. 5. **ATM Withdrawals**: Using your debit card to withdraw cash from local ATMs is an option, but be aware of international withdrawal fees and always choose ATMs from reputable banks.
