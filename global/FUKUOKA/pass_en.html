Fukuoka Passes FAQ

Q: What is the best way to get from Fukuoka Airport to Hakata Station or Tenjin?

Taking the Fukuoka City Subway Kuko Line is highly recommended! Boarding directly from the Domestic Terminal, it takes just 5 minutes to reach Hakata Station and 11 minutes to reach Tenjin—making it one of the most conveniently located city-center airports in the world. If arriving at the International Terminal, you can take a free shuttle bus (approx. 10–15 minutes) to the Domestic Terminal first, or hop on a direct express bus to Hakata/Tenjin right from the International Terminal.

Q: Can I use Suica or PASMO cards on public transit in Fukuoka?

Yes! Fukuoka’s Nishitetsu and subway systems fully support nationwide Japanese IC cards. You can use Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, as well as local passes like nimoca, SUGOCA, and Hayakaken for subways, buses, and JR trains.

Q: Is it true that I can tap my contactless credit card to ride the Fukuoka Subway?

Yes! All stations on the Fukuoka City Subway fully support contactless card payments (including VISA, JCB, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay). Plus, it automatically caps your daily spending at ¥640, so any rides beyond that amount are essentially free for the rest of the day.

Q: How do I travel between the International and Domestic terminals at Fukuoka Airport?

There is a free terminal shuttle bus connecting the terminals. Buses run frequently (every 5–8 minutes), and the ride takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
