✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Subway Connection (Fukuoka City Subway Kuko Line)

The Domestic Terminal of Fukuoka Airport (FUK) is directly connected underground to Fukuoka Airport Station (K08). If you arrive at the International Terminal, simply take the free airport shuttle bus to the Domestic Terminal first, then hop on the Subway Kuko Line. It is incredibly convenient—just 2 stops (about 5 minutes) to Hakata Station and 5 stops (about 11 minutes) to Tenjin Station!

📍 Boarding at:

Take the free shuttle bus from Bus Stop No. 1 outside the International Arrivals Hall -> Get off at the Domestic Terminal -> Follow the signs to the B1 floor for Fukuoka Airport Subway Station.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

You can buy a single-ride ticket at the vending machines (¥260 to Hakata), use an IC card (Suica, ICOCA, nimoca, etc.), or simply tap a contactless credit card or mobile wallet at the ticket gates.

Recommended Direct Airport Bus (Nishitetsu Bus)

Express and city buses depart directly from Fukuoka Airport International Terminal to Hakata Station, the Tenjin area, Dazaifu, and other top destinations across Kyushu (such as Yufuin, Kurokawa Onsen, and Beppu). It is a hassle-free option that saves you from lugging heavy bags onto transfer shuttles.

📍 Boarding at:

Bus stops are located outside the 1F exit of the International Terminal (Bus Stop #2 for Hakata/Tenjin; Bus Stop #3 for Dazaifu and long-distance express buses).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

For city buses, tap your IC card or credit card when boarding, or take a numbered transit slip and pay with cash when getting off. For long-distance highway buses, booking tickets in advance online or at the Nishitetsu Bus counter is recommended.

Recommended Taxi Services

Because Fukuoka Airport is exceptionally close to the downtown area, a taxi ride to Hakata Station takes only about 15 minutes (costing around ¥1,500–¥2,000), and about 20 minutes to Tenjin (costing around ¥2,500–¥3,500). If you have bulky luggage or are traveling with a group of 3–4 people, taking a taxi is effortless and very cost-effective.

📍 Boarding at:

Designated taxi stands located outside the 1F exit of both the International and Domestic Terminals.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Fares are metered. You can pay with cash, credit card, or mobile payment options when you reach your destination.
