✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Yangon International Airport (RGN) Train Transfer Guide

Yangon International Airport currently does not have a direct rail connection to its terminals. To travel into the city center by train, you'll first need to take a short 10-15 minute taxi ride to the nearest Yangon Circular Railway station (such as Paung Daw Thi or Wai Lu Wun Station), where you can catch a train to Yangon Central Railway Station.

📍 Boarding at:

Take a taxi from the airport Arrivals Hall to the nearest train station, then wait for your train on the platform.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Train tickets must be purchased in person at the station ticket window. Only Myanmar Kyat (MMK) cash is accepted.

Recommended YBS Airport Bus (Line 37)

The Yangon Bus Service (YBS) operates a route directly connecting Yangon International Airport with the city center, including key landmarks like Sule Pagoda and Yangon Central Railway Station. Travel time is approximately 60 to 90 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.

📍 Boarding at:

Board at the dedicated airport bus shelter located just outside the Terminal Arrival Hall exit.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay cash upon boarding (around 500 MMK per person; exact change required as no change is given) or tap a YPS card.

Recommended Airport Taxis & Grab Rideshare

This is the most convenient, comfortable, and reliable option for traveling between the airport and downtown. You can get an upfront fare by booking via the Grab app, or arrange a ride at the official taxi counter in the Arrivals Hall. The journey to downtown takes about 45-60 minutes.

📍 Boarding at:

Head to the designated taxi pickup area or Grab pickup point right outside the Arrivals Hall exit.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Grab allows payment via credit card linked in the app or cash in MMK. Official airport taxi counters accept cash payments in MMK at the desk.
