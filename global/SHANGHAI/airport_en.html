✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Metro & Maglev Transfer Guide (Pudong PVG / Hongqiao SHA)

[Pudong Airport PVG]: Choose between the thrilling Shanghai Maglev (whisking you to Longyang Road Station in just 8 minutes) or Metro Line 2 (direct to People's Square and East Nanjing Road in about 60 minutes). [Hongqiao Airport SHA]: Take Metro Line 10 from Terminal 1, or Lines 2 and 10 from Terminal 2 for direct access to downtown and the Hongqiao High-Speed Railway Station.

📍 Boarding at:

[Pudong Airport]: 2nd Floor, Transportation Center between T1 and T2; [Hongqiao Airport]: Underground transit levels at T1 and T2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay using WeChat/Alipay travel codes ('Suishenma' or 'Ride Code'), Apple Pay transit cards, or buy single-ride, 1-day, or 3-day passes at ticket machines. Show your same-day flight ticket to enjoy a 20% discount on the Maglev (RMB 40 single trip).

Recommended Airport Express Buses (Shuttles & Night Lines)

[Pudong Airport]: Features Express Line 1 (to Hongqiao Hub), Line 2 (to Jing'an Temple), Line 4 (to Luxun Park), Line 9 (to Xinzhuang), and Night Lines (operating after metro hours until the last flight lands). [Hongqiao Airport]: Offers overnight shuttles and several express routes connecting directly to downtown.

📍 Boarding at:

[Pudong Airport]: Outer bus hub platforms on the T1/T2 Arrivals level; [Hongqiao Airport]: Bus Terminal at the East Transportation Center, Terminal 2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Buy tickets at station windows or self-service kiosks, or pay using a Shanghai Public Transportation Card or WeChat Mini Program.

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing (DiDi / Amap)

Official Shanghai taxis (Dazhong, Qiangsheng, Jinjiang, Haibo) use meters with a 30% surcharge late at night (23:00–05:00). A ride to downtown People's Square costs around RMB 150–200 from Pudong Airport and RMB 60–90 from Hongqiao Airport. App-based ride-hailing services pick up at designated parking areas.

📍 Boarding at:

[Pudong Airport]: Official taxi queues outside T1/T2 Arrivals; Ride-hailing pickup at designated zones in P1/P2 parking garages. [Hongqiao Airport]: Dedicated ride-hailing and taxi pickup spots in P6/P7 parking garages at Terminal 2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Cash, WeChat Pay, and Alipay are accepted.
