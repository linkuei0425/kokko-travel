Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwan residents must hold a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (Taibaozheng). If you haven't applied for one or if your permit has expired, you can conveniently apply for a single-entry permit upon arrival at the border inspection counters at Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG) or Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). Just remember to bring your original Taiwan passport, original National ID card, and two 2-inch color photos with a white background.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Strictly prohibited items include animals, plants, and their products, fresh fruits, meat products (such as pork jerky, meat floss, and sausages), illegal drugs, weapons, and politically sensitive publications. If you are carrying more than RMB 20,000 in cash or foreign currency exceeding USD 5,000 equivalent, you must make a written declaration to customs.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

For Taiwan passport holders clearing customs with a Taibaozheng: 1. [Paper Arrival Card]: You will be using the Chinese Citizen border control lane, so you absolutely DO NOT need to fill out a Foreigner Arrival Card. 2. [Online Declaration System]: The previously required Health Declaration Card (Customs Black Code) was officially abolished as of November 1, 2023, meaning regular travelers do not need to fill out an online health declaration in advance. However, if you have items that need to be declared, please head to the 'Goods to Declare' (Red Channel) to complete your declaration on-site.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB), represented by the symbol Yuan (￥).

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China (Shanghai Branch - Bund Sub-branch): Offers the best exchange rates without high service fees. Located at No. 23 Zhongshan East 1st Road, Huangpu District. Getting there: Take Metro Line 2 or 10 to 'East Nanjing Road Station', head east along East Nanjing Road for about an 8-minute walk. It's right near the iconic Peace Hotel on The Bund. 2. Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC - East Nanjing Road Sub-branch): Located at No. 99 East Nanjing Road, Huangpu District. Getting there: Take Metro Line 2 or 10 to 'East Nanjing Road Station' Exit 7, and walk for about 3 minutes. It's located right near Bund Central Plaza.

💳 Payment Tips

1. Mainland China is largely a cashless society. Before your trip, it is highly recommended to download WeChat Pay or Alipay and link your credit card so you can easily scan to pay anywhere. 2. When exchanging currency over the counter at a physical bank, be sure to bring your original Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (or official travel ID/passport) for identity verification. 3. Airport currency exchange counters (like Travelex) charge hefty service fees (around 50–60 RMB per transaction), so they are best reserved for urgent, small-amount exchanges.
