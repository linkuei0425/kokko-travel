Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Great news for Taiwanese passport holders! You can enjoy visa-free entry to Japan for up to 90 days for short-term tourism, visiting relatives, or business trips. Just ensure your passport is valid upon entry—having at least 6 months of remaining validity is highly recommended.

🛃 Customs Declarations

To ensure a seamless arrival, please note that meat and meat products (including jerky, sausages, and canned meats), as well as fresh fruits and vegetables, are strictly prohibited. Carrying cash or equivalent foreign currency exceeding 1 million JPY requires a customs declaration. Pay special attention if you are bringing gold products, as strict weight limits and declaration procedures apply.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Entering Japan is easier than ever! Taiwanese travelers can choose between traditional paper forms or convenient online declarations. Flight attendants will hand out paper arrival cards and customs declaration forms during your flight, or you can register your details ahead of time using Japan's official 'Visit Japan Web (VJW)' service. Once completed, you'll receive QR codes for immigration and customs. Upon arriving at international airports such as New Chitose or Hakodate, simply scan the QR codes on your phone to breeze through border control—no paper required!

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Japanese Yen (JPY / ¥)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. World Currency Shop (Sapporo Branch): Conveniently located on the 1st floor of the Sapporo Terminal Building, just a 2-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Sapporo Station (near ESTA and Bic Camera), offering transparent and reliable exchange rates. 2. 7-Bank ATMs (e.g., 7-Eleven Sapporo Station North Exit Branch): Right outside the JR Sapporo Station North Exit. You can easily withdraw Japanese Yen cash 24/7 using an internationally enabled debit or credit card with low transaction fees.

💳 Payment Tips

While major department stores, JR stations, chain hotels, and restaurants across Hokkaido widely accept credit cards and Apple Pay, cash is still king when exploring remote attractions, visiting farms in Furano and Biei, dining at traditional ramen shops, or riding local buses. We recommend exchanging some Yen before your trip. If you need quick cash on the go, 7-Bank ATMs located in 7-Eleven stores throughout Hokkaido offer the most convenient cash withdrawal option.
