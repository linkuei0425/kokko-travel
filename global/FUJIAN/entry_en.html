Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers from Taiwan must hold a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (Taibaozheng). If you haven't applied for one in advance, you can conveniently obtain a single-entry Taibaozheng (visa on arrival) upon entry at major ports in Fujian Province, such as Xiamen Gaoqi International Airport, Fuzhou Changle International Airport, and Xiamen Wutong Passenger Wharf. Simply bring your Taiwanese passport, original national ID card, a 2-inch color photo with a white background, and the application fee of around RMB 40.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Bringing plants, animals and their products, fresh meat, uninspected fruits, fresh produce, and illegal drugs into the country is strictly prohibited. Duty-free allowances per person permit up to 400 cigarettes and 1,500 ml of alcoholic beverages (above 12% ABV). The cash limit is set at RMB 20,000 or foreign currency equivalent to USD 5,000; any amount exceeding this limit must be declared to customs.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Good news for Taiwanese passport and Taibaozheng holders: when entering Fujian by air or via the Mini Three Links, you do NOT need to fill out a paper Arrival Card. Additionally, routine health declarations via the 'Customs Pocket Service' have been discontinued. Simply head straight to the dedicated 'Hong Kong, Macao, & Taiwan / Taibaozheng' lanes with your valid permit for quick scanning or manual inspection—no prior online electronic arrival declaration is required!

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

For the best currency exchange experience, head to either the Bank of China Fuzhou Branch (No. 153 Wuyi North Road, Gulou District, Fuzhou — a 5-minute walk from Exit 1 of Shuibu Metro Station on Line 2, near Sanfang Qixiang and Wuyi Square) or the Bank of China Xiamen Branch (No. 98 Lujiang Road, Siming District, Xiamen — an 8-minute walk from Exit 1 of Zhenhai Road Metro Station on Line 1, right across from the Xiamen Ferry Terminal). As the most comprehensive major bank branches for foreign exchange in Fujian Province, both offer the best official rates and cash exchange services for New Taiwan Dollars (TWD) and other foreign currencies.

💳 Payment Tips

Daily spending in Fujian relies heavily on digital payments (WeChat Pay and Alipay). Be sure to download these apps and link your credit or debit card before setting off. However, it is still wise to keep a small amount of RMB cash on hand—either exchanged at Bank of China or withdrawn directly from an ATM using an international debit card—just in case you visit small street vendors or encounter poor network coverage.
