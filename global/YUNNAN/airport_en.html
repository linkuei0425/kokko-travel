✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Kunming Changshui Airport Metro & High-Speed Rail Connections (Metro Line 6)

Kunming Changshui International Airport offers seamless access to the city via Metro Line 6. Hop on Line 6 and transfer at Juhua Station to Line 4 to reach Kunming South Railway Station (for high-speed train connections), or ride to Tangzixiang Station and switch to Line 2 to go directly into the heart of downtown Kunming (Dongfeng Square and Nanping Street).

📍 Boarding at:

Changshui Airport Metro Station on Level B2 of the airport terminal.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Single-journey tickets can be easily bought from self-service ticket machines inside the station, or you can conveniently scan the 'Kunming Metro Pass' code using WeChat or Alipay to pass through the gates.

Recommended Airport Express Bus (Konggang Kuaixian)

The Airport Express Bus offers direct routes to major city landmarks. Line 1 takes you to Xiaoximen and the New Xinhua Hotel; Line 2 connects directly to Kunming Railway Station and Jinjiang Hotel, alongside other routes bound for local long-distance bus stations. Buses depart every 15–30 minutes, with operational hours matched to flight arrival schedules.

📍 Boarding at:

Dedicated Airport Bus Waiting Area located right outside the Arrivals Hall on Level 1 of the terminal.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

You can purchase tickets at the Airport Express ticket counter inside the Arrivals Hall, or book them online via the 'Konggang Kuaixian' WeChat Official Account.

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing Services (DiDi)

Available 24/7 for round-the-clock convenience. A ride to downtown Kunming (around Dongfeng Square) takes approximately 40–50 minutes and costs roughly RMB 80–120 (including highway tolls). Ride-hailing services operate from a dedicated pickup zone.

📍 Boarding at:

For traditional taxis, head to the designated taxi queue outside the Arrivals Hall on Level 1. For ride-hailing services (such as DiDi), proceed to the dedicated ride-hailing pickup area in the Level B1 parking garage.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Taxis are metered (nighttime surcharges apply). For ride-hailing, simply book and pay online via the DiDi app or its WeChat/Alipay mini-programs.
