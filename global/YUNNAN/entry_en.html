Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers from Taiwan visiting Yunnan must hold a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (Taibaozheng). You can easily apply for this through major travel agencies in Taiwan. No additional Chinese visa is required, and the permit allows for multiple entries and stays throughout its validity period.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Each passenger is allowed to carry up to RMB 20,000 in cash, and foreign currency equivalent to no more than USD 5,000 without declaring it. Strictly prohibited items include fresh fruit, plants, animals and their products, meat products (such as bird's nest and sausages), illegal drugs, and printed materials containing politically sensitive content.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Taiwanese travelers entering Yunnan (e.g., via Kunming Changshui International Airport) on international or direct cross-strait flights with a Taibaozheng do not need to complete a paper arrival card. Routine health declarations are currently suspended. However, if you are carrying items that require declaration, you can use the WeChat mini-program "Customs Pocket Service" to submit an electronic form ahead of time, or fill out a paper form via the Red Channel at customs clearance.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China (Kunming Zhengyi Road Sub-branch): Located at No. 106 Zhengyi Road, Wuhua District, Kunming (near Kunming Old Street and Zhengyifang Shopping Center). Take Kunming Metro Line 3 to Wuyi Road Station (Exit B) and walk about 500 meters. This branch is highly recommended for foreign currency cash exchange services. 2. Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC - Kunming Nanping Sub-branch): Located in the vibrant Nanping Pedestrian Street area downtown (near the Jinma Biji Pavilion commercial district). Take Metro Line 1 or 2 to Dongfeng Square Station (Exit H) and walk roughly 600 meters. The branch features dedicated currency exchange counters with fully transparent rates.

💳 Payment Tips

Mainland China is largely a cashless society, with daily transactions dominated by Alipay and WeChat Pay. Before your trip, download these apps, complete real-name identity verification, and link your credit or debit card. You will only need to prepare around 500 to 1,000 RMB in cash as emergency backup funds. Keep in mind that exchanging currency at bank branches requires presenting your original valid identity document (such as a Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents or a Passport).
