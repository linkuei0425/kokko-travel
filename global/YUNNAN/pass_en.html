Yunnan Passes FAQ

Q: What is the fastest way to get from Kunming Changshui Airport to Kunming South High-Speed Railway Station?

The best option is taking Kunming Metro Line 6 to Juhua Station, then transferring to Line 4 directly to Kunming South Station. The entire trip takes about 50–60 minutes, helping you avoid city traffic entirely.

Q: Can I use mobile payment to take buses and subways in Yunnan?

Yes! Search for the 'Transit Code' mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, enable the 'Kunming Metro Code' or 'Yunnan Transit Code', and simply scan the QR code on your phone to board buses or enter metro stations.

Q: Where can I buy the Kunming Metro 1-Day Pass, and how does it work?

Physical 1-Day Passes (15 RMB) and 3-Day Passes (40 RMB) can be purchased at customer service centers in any Kunming Metro station. They offer unlimited rides across the entire subway network (including the Airport Line) for 24 hours (or 72 hours) starting from your first entry.

Q: What is the recommended mode of transport between major Yunnan cities (e.g., Kunming, Dali, Lijiang, Xishuangbanna)?

Yunnan has an extensive railway network, making high-speed trains (bullet trains) the best choice between cities. Travel times from Kunming are roughly 2 hours to Dali, 3.5 hours to Lijiang, and 3 to 4 hours to Xishuangbanna—making it fast, comfortable, and reliable.
