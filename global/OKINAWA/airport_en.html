✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Naha Airport Monorail (Yui Rail)

While Naha Airport doesn't have a conventional railway system, it is directly connected to the Okinawa Urban Monorail, fondly known as the Yui Rail. A 2nd-floor pedestrian bridge from the Domestic Terminal takes you straight to Naha Airport Station. Taking the monorail provides effortless access to key downtown hubs, including Kokusai-dori (Prefectural Office and Makishi Stations), Shinjoshin (Omoromachi Station), and Shuri Castle (Shuri Station).

📍 Boarding at:

Walk across the 2nd-floor pedestrian bridge from the Domestic Terminal departures lobby—it is just a quick 3-minute stroll to the Naha Airport Station platform.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Purchase single-ride tickets (QR code tickets), 1-Day Passes, or 2-Day Passes at the station's automatic ticket machines. Alternatively, you can simply tap through the gates using major Japanese IC cards like Suica, ICOCA, or the local OKICA card.

Recommended Airport Limousine Bus, Express Bus & Local Route Buses

Buses provide direct transportation to major resort hotels and top attractions across central and northern Okinawa. Options include the Airport Limousine Bus serving beachfront resorts in Onna Village and Nago; the Yanbaru Express Bus and Highway Express Buses (No. 111/117) heading straight to Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium; and regular local route buses (such as No. 23 and 120) bound for downtown Naha and central destinations.

📍 Boarding at:

Bus stops are located right outside the 1st-floor Arrivals Hall of both Domestic and International Terminals (Limousine and Highway Express Buses generally depart from stops 2 through 4).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

For the Limousine Bus, purchase tickets in advance at the service counter or dedicated ticket machines on the 1st floor of the Domestic Terminal. For express and local buses, take a numbered ticket upon boarding and pay with cash when exiting, or use an accepted travel pass.

Recommended Taxis, Charters & Ride-Hailing Apps

Taxis are readily available right outside the airport terminals. Reaching Kokusai-dori in downtown Naha takes about 15 to 20 minutes and costs around ¥1,500 to ¥2,000. On-site taxi ranks are divided into separate queues for short-distance (within Naha City) and medium-to-long-distance trips. You can also easily hail or pre-book a ride using the DiDi or GO apps.

📍 Boarding at:

Head to the taxi stands located outside the 1st-floor exits of both Domestic and International Terminals (clearly separated into short-distance and medium/long-distance queues).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay upon arrival using cash, credit card, or mobile payment apps (such as PayPay, depending on the decals displayed on the vehicle).
