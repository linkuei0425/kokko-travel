Route Hong Kong - Macau

Recommended Transport

Embark on a thrilling high-speed ferry journey with TurboJET or Cotai Water Jet.

Boarding Location & Transfer Guide

In Hong Kong, you have two primary ferry terminals for your adventure to Macau: 1. Hong Kong-Macau Ferry Terminal (Sheung Wan): Take the MTR to Sheung Wan Station, exit D, and enjoy a pleasant 5-minute stroll to the 3rd floor of Shun Tak Centre. This is the busiest terminal, offering frequent departures. 2. China Ferry Terminal (Tsim Sha Tsui): Hop on the MTR to Tsim Sha Tsui Station, exit A1, and walk for about 10 minutes to reach the terminal nestled within Harbour City. This terminal has fewer sailings. Upon arrival in Macau, TurboJET ferries typically dock at the Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal, while Cotai Water Jet ferries usually arrive at the Taipa Ferry Terminal.

Schedule & Operating Hours

Both ferry operators boast frequent departures, with services running approximately every 15-30 minutes during the day. Night-time sailings are less frequent. The first ferry typically departs around 07:00, with the last departures between 00:00 and 01:00. Some overnight services are also available during specific periods. We highly recommend checking the latest schedules online and booking your tickets in advance, especially if you're traveling on weekends or public holidays, to secure your spot.

Route Hong Kong - Shenzhen/Guangzhou (Mainland China)

Recommended Transport

Experience the lightning-fast High-Speed Rail (HSR).

Boarding Location & Transfer Guide

Hong Kong West Kowloon Station: Reach this impressive station by taking the MTR to Austin Station (Exit F) and enjoying a short 5-minute walk. Alternatively, take the MTR to Kowloon Station (Exit C) for an 8-10 minute walk. West Kowloon Station operates under a convenient 'co-location arrangement,' allowing you to complete both Hong Kong and Mainland China immigration procedures within the same station, streamlining your journey.

Schedule & Operating Hours

Services are incredibly frequent, especially for Shenzhen Futian/North stations, with trains departing approximately every 10-20 minutes during the day. There are also numerous services to Guangzhou South. The first train typically departs around 07:00, with the last departures between 22:00 and 23:00. To ensure a smooth trip, we highly recommend purchasing your tickets in advance via the 12306 website or at the station counter, particularly during public holidays.

Route Hong Kong - Shenzhen (Mainland China)

Recommended Transport

Take the convenient MTR East Rail Line.

Boarding Location & Transfer Guide

Simply board the MTR East Rail Line and ride it to either Lo Wu Station or Lok Ma Chau Station. The Lo Wu border crossing connects directly to Shenzhen's bustling Lo Wu Commercial City, while the Lok Ma Chau crossing leads to Shenzhen's Futian Port. Both stations offer convenient immigration channels.

Schedule & Operating Hours

The East Rail Line operates with impressive frequency, with trains running approximately every 3-8 minutes during the day. The first train typically departs between 05:30 and 06:00, with the last departures between 23:00 and 00:00. Border crossing points are generally open from 06:30 to 00:00, so please keep these operating hours in mind for your journey.
