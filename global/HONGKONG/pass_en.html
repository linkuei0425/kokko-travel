Best Value Strategy Tips

💡 **Top Value Tip:** We recommend purchasing an **Octopus Card** upon arrival and loading it with approximately $200-300 HKD for daily transportation and convenience store purchases. If your itinerary includes the Airport Express and frequent MTR rides, consider adding the **Airport Express Travel Pass**. Opt for the round-trip option and activate the 72-hour unlimited MTR feature on your first day to maximize its benefits. Also, keep an eye out for any **Hong Kong Goodies Visitor Spending Vouchers** offered by the Hong Kong Tourism Board, as these can provide significant additional savings!
