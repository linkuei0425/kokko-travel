✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Express

The rapid rail link that whisks you directly from Hong Kong International Airport (Chek Lap Kok) to the city center, making stops at Tsing Yi, Kowloon, and Hong Kong stations. With a journey time of approximately 24 minutes, it's undoubtedly the fastest way to reach the heart of the city.

📍 Boarding at:

Upon arrival at Hong Kong International Airport, simply follow the 'Airport Express' signs. The Airport Express platforms are conveniently located at the Ground Transportation Centre (GTC) of Terminal 1. Trains run frequently, with departures every 10-12 minutes, ensuring minimal waiting time.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Multiple ways to purchase your ticket: 1. Airport Express Counters: Find them conveniently located in the airport's arrival hall and at all Airport Express stations. You can pay with cash, credit card, or an Octopus card. 2. Octopus Card: Simply tap your Octopus card directly at the gate for automatic fare deduction. If you have an Airport Express Travel Pass, you can use it directly. 3. Automatic Ticket Machines: Available at all stations, these machines accept cash and credit cards. 4. Online Purchase/Platforms like Klook: For potential discounts and convenience, consider buying e-tickets in advance through travel platforms like Klook or Trip.com. You can often use a QR code for direct entry or exchange it for a physical ticket at the counter. 5. Group Tickets: Traveling with companions? Groups of 2 or more can enjoy special discounts on group tickets.

Recommended Airport Buses (Citybus / Long Win Bus)

Offering numerous routes that connect the airport to various districts across Hong Kong. While generally more affordable than the Airport Express, the journey time is longer. Buses are equipped with dedicated luggage racks, making them a convenient option for travelers with larger bags.

📍 Boarding at:

Upon arrival at Hong Kong International Airport, follow the 'Bus' signs to the Ground Transportation Centre (GTC). The bus stops are located just outside Terminal 1's Ground Transportation Centre. Each bus route features clear signboards indicating its destination.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Ticket purchase options: 1. Octopus Card: Simply tap your Octopus card upon boarding, and the fare will be automatically deducted. 2. Cash: Pay with exact change by dropping coins into the fare box when you board. Please note that no change will be given. 3. Bus Ticket Offices: Located at the Ground Transportation Centre, where you can purchase single journey tickets or day passes (for selected routes). 4. Online Purchase: Some bus companies offer the convenience of online ticket purchasing.

Recommended Taxis

Offering 24-hour service, taxis provide direct access to your destination, making them ideal for travelers with heavy luggage or those arriving late at night. Hong Kong taxis come in three distinct colors, each serving different areas: Red (Urban areas), Green (New Territories), and Blue (Lantau Island). At the airport, you'll find only Red and Green taxis available.

📍 Boarding at:

Upon arrival at Hong Kong International Airport, follow the 'Taxi' signs to the taxi stands at the Ground Transportation Centre (GTC). The taxi stands are well-marked with clear signs guiding travelers to the waiting areas for different colored taxis.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Payment methods: 1. Cash: Hong Kong taxis widely accept cash payments, so it's advisable to have sufficient small change ready. 2. Octopus Card: Some taxis now accept Octopus card payments; it's a good idea to ask the driver before you start your journey. 3. Credit Card/E-payment: An increasing number of taxis are accepting credit cards or e-payment options (such as Alipay and WeChat Pay), but this is not universally available. It's best to confirm with the driver before boarding. Fares: Fares are charged according to the meter, with additional surcharges for luggage, tunnel tolls, and cross-harbour fees. Always ask the driver for a receipt.
