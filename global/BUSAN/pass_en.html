Busan Passes FAQ

Q: Does the VISIT BUSAN PASS include free rides on the Busan Metro and public buses?

No, it does not. While the physical VISIT BUSAN PASS card features a built-in T-Money chip, the pass price only covers attraction entries. You must load cash onto the card at a convenience store or subway station before using it for subways, buses, or light rail.

Q: Can I use the Busan Metro 1-Day Pass on the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail or the Donghae Line?

No. The Busan Metro 1-Day Pass is strictly valid for Lines 1 through 4 operated by Busan Transportation Corporation. It cannot be used on the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail, the Donghae Line metro/commuter rail, or city buses.

Q: Are there transfer discounts between the metro and buses in Busan?

Yes! As long as you use a reloadable transit card (like T-Money or Cashbee) and transfer to another bus or metro within 30 minutes of tapping out, you will receive a transfer discount (valid for up to 2 consecutive transfers).
