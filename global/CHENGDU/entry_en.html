Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers from Taiwan must hold a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (commonly known as the Taibaozheng). If you don't have one, don't worry—you can easily apply for a single-entry permit upon arrival at the Port Visa Office in Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (T1/T2) or Shuangliu International Airport. Just make sure to bring your original Taiwan ID card, passport, a 2-inch color photo with a white background, and the application fee of approximately RMB 40.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Strictly prohibited items include illegal publications, animal and plant products, fresh meat, fruits, seeds, drugs, and firearms or ammunition. Additionally, if you are carrying more than RMB 20,000 in cash or foreign currency equivalents exceeding USD 5,000 upon entry, you must proactively declare it in writing to customs.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Great news for Taiwan passport holders entering with a Taibaozheng: You do NOT need to fill out a paper Foreigner Arrival Card when boarding or clearing customs. Furthermore, China Customs has officially phased out the Health Declaration Code ('Black Code'). Standard travelers no longer need to complete an online health form on their phones—simply present your Taibaozheng at the manual counters or automated e-channels to breeze through immigration!

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China - Chengdu Jinjiang Branch (No. 15 Zongfu Road): Located in the bustling Chunxi Road shopping district, right near iconic landmarks like Sino-Ocean Taikoo Li Chengdu and Chengdu IFS. Getting there: Take Chengdu Metro Line 2 or 3 to Chunxi Road Station (Exit D) and walk about 5 minutes. This branch offers foreign currency exchange with competitive, transparent rates. 2. Travelex Currency Exchange (Tianfu International Airport): Located near the International/Hong Kong, Macao & Taiwan Arrivals exit on the Arrivals floor of Terminal 2. Perfect if you need a quick cash exchange right after landing.

💳 Payment Tips

Mobile payments are king in Chengdu! We strongly recommend using Alipay or WeChat Pay, both of which support linking international credit or debit cards for seamless QR-code payments. You only need a small amount of cash (around 300–500 RMB) as a backup, as many local shops rarely have change for physical banknotes.
