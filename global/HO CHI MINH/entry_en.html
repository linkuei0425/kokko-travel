Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers holding a Taiwan passport visiting Vietnam must apply in advance for an official Vietnam Electronic Visa (e-Visa) or obtain a Visa on Arrival Approval Letter via a travel agency. You can easily apply for the e-Visa online through the Vietnam Immigration Department website. Available for single or multiple entries, it allows a stay of up to 90 days with a processing time of about 3 to 5 business days. Upon arrival, simply present a printed copy of your e-Visa alongside your passport, which must be valid for at least 6 months.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Your duty-free allowances include up to 200 cigarettes and 1.5 liters of spirits (above 20% ABV). If you are carrying cash exceeding US$5,000 (or the foreign currency equivalent) or 15 million Vietnamese Dong (VND), you must declare it to customs. Please note that uninspected fresh or processed meat products (including meat snacks and instant noodles with meat packets), plants, illegal drugs, controlled weapons, and unauthorized publications are strictly prohibited.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

For travelers holding a Taiwan passport entering via Ho Chi Minh City's Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN): 1. [Paper Arrival Card]: Vietnam has fully phased out physical arrival cards, so you do NOT need to fill out any paper forms during your flight or at border control. 2. [Online Electronic Declaration]: Vietnam currently does not require mandatory online arrival declarations for general tourists. Simply prepare your valid passport, a printed physical copy of your e-Visa featuring its QR code, and proof of return flight booking, and you can proceed directly to immigration and customs inspection.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Vietnamese Dong (VND)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

The top-recommended currency exchange spots with the best rates in Ho Chi Minh City are two famous gold shops located right opposite the West Gate of Ben Thanh Market: 1. 【Ha Tam Jewelry (Tiệm Vàng Hà Tâm)】: Boasts some of the best exchange rates in town and draws the biggest crowds. Nearby landmarks include the West Gate of Ben Thanh Market and Ben Thanh Station. How to get there: Take a Grab or bus to Ben Thanh Bus Station and walk for about 1 minute (located near the intersection of Nguyễn An Ninh and Phan Chu Trinh streets). 2. 【Mai Van Jewelry (Tiệm Vàng Mai Vân)】: Located right next to Ha Tam, offering virtually identical exchange rates with slightly shorter queues—another highly reputable choice.

💳 Payment Tips

1. Bring crisp, clean, and uncreased $100 USD bills (issued in 2013 or later) to the gold shops for the best exchange rate. 2. The gold shops around Ben Thanh Market also accept New Taiwan Dollar (TWD) cash directly into Vietnamese Dong at quite favorable rates. 3. Currency exchange counters inside Tan Son Nhat Airport offer less favorable rates; it is recommended to exchange just a small amount ($20–$50 USD) at the airport to cover your transport into the city, and exchange the rest at downtown gold shops.
