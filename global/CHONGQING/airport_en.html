✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Rail Transit & High-Speed Rail Connections

Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG) offers direct access to the Chongqing Rail Transit network: Line 3 and Line 10 serve Terminal 2 (T2), while Line 10 serves Terminal 3 (T3). Line 10 provides seamless direct links to major high-speed rail hubs, including Chongqing North Railway Station and Chongqing West Railway Station, making onward travel to other cities effortless. To reach the bustling Jiefangbei Commercial District, simply take Line 10 to Hongtudi Station and transfer to Line 6 heading to Xiaoshizi Station.

📍 Boarding at:

Terminal 2: Follow the signs to the B2 Metro Station entrance (Line 3 / Line 10). Terminal 3: Proceed to levels B1/B2 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC) to catch Line 10.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Single-journey tickets can be purchased at automated ticket machines, or you can scan in directly using the 'Chongqing Rail Transit Travel Code' via WeChat or Alipay.

Recommended Airport Express Bus

Jiangbei Airport operates a convenient network of Airport Express Bus routes (K01–K07) with frequent departures. Route K01 is the most popular option, connecting Terminals 2 and 3 directly to the heart of downtown at Jiefangbei (near Guotai Arts Center and Hongyadong). The full ride takes approximately 60 minutes.

📍 Boarding at:

Terminal 2: Outside the 1st Floor Arrivals Exit. Terminal 3: Bus terminal on the 1st Floor of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Tickets are available at on-site ticket windows, self-service kiosks, or online via the 'Chongqing Passenger Transport' WeChat official account. A single ticket costs around 15 RMB.

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing Services (DiDi)

Standard taxis—affectionately dubbed 'Yellow Ferraris' by locals—run on meters. A ride to downtown Jiefangbei costs around 50–70 RMB and takes 30–40 minutes, depending on traffic. Ride-hailing services like DiDi feature upfront pricing and dedicated pickup zones at the airport.

📍 Boarding at:

Standard Taxis: Outside 1st Floor Arrivals at T2, or 1st Floor GTC at T3. Ride-Hailing: Dedicated pickup area in the underground parking garage of the T3 Ground Transportation Center (GTC).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Standard taxis are metered (payment via Alipay, WeChat Pay, or cash). Ride-hailing fares are paid automatically through mobile apps like DiDi.
