Chongqing Passes FAQ

Q: Can I use WeChat or Alipay QR codes to ride the Chongqing metro and buses?

Yes! Simply search for the 'Transport Code' (乘車碼) mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, set your location to Chongqing, and activate the Chongqing Rail Transit and Bus QR codes. You can then tap your phone screen at turnstiles for seamless entry.

Q: Which line should I take to see the famous 'Train Passing Through a Building' at Liziba?

Take CRT Line 2 and get off at Liziba Station. Stay inside the train to experience riding right through the residential building, or head out Exit 1 to the ground-level viewing platform for the iconic photo of the monorail entering the building.

Q: Is it better to take the metro or a taxi from Jiangbei International Airport to Jiefangbei?

Taking rail transit is the most time-efficient option and avoids Chongqing's notorious traffic jams. From the airport, take Line 10 to Zengjiayan Station and transfer to Line 2, or take Line 10 to Hongtudi Station and transfer to Line 6 to Xiaoshizi Station (right next to Jiefangbei and Hongyadong). The journey takes around 45–60 minutes.

Q: Do I need to buy Yangtze River Cableway tickets in advance? Are transit passes accepted?

We strongly recommend reserving your spot and buying tickets in advance via the official 'Yangtze River Cableway' WeChat mini-program to avoid massive queues. You can also tap the physical Yiju Changtong Card at the turnstile for a special discount, but please note that the 1-Day and 3-Day Rail Passes are NOT accepted.
