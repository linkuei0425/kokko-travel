Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers from Taiwan must hold a valid Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (commonly known as the Taiwan Compatriot Permit). If you don't have one, you can apply for a single-entry permit (visa on arrival) at Terminal 3 of Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG). To do so, bring your original Taiwan passport, original Taiwan ID card, two 2-inch color photos with a white background, and the processing fee of approximately RMB 40. However, to ensure a smooth journey, we strongly recommend obtaining a 5-year permit in Taiwan prior to your trip for visa-free entry.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Strict regulations prohibit bringing meat products (including pork and processed meats), fresh fruits, as well as live animals, plants, and their products into the country. If you are carrying cash exceeding the equivalent of USD 5,000 in foreign currency or RMB 20,000, you must declare it to customs upon arrival. Standard duty-free allowances apply for tobacco and alcohol (up to 400 cigarettes and 1.5 liters of alcoholic beverages).

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Good news for Taiwan travelers holding a Taiwan Compatriot Permit: you do NOT need to fill out a paper arrival card when flying to or entering Chongqing. Furthermore, as of November 1, 2023, China Customs officially phased out the Health Declaration Form (formerly known as the 'Customs Black Code'), so no online electronic declaration is required. Upon arriving at Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport, simply head straight to the 'Chinese Citizens / Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao' lane, present your permit at the counter, or use the automated e-gates for quick and easy entry.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China Chongqing Branch Business Department: Located in the heart of the central Jiefangbei Commercial District (Address: No. 188 Minzu Road, Yuzhong District, Chongqing). Getting there: Take Chongqing Metro Line 1 or Line 6 to Xiaoshizi Station, take Exit 6, and walk for about 3 minutes. It is conveniently situated near famous landmarks like Luohan Temple and Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street. Exchange rates are publicly displayed with full transparency and zero extra service fees. 2. Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC) Chongqing Jiefangbei Sub-branch (Address: No. 27 Minquan Road, Yuzhong District, Chongqing). Getting there: Take Chongqing Metro Line 2 to Linjiangmen Station, take Exit B, and walk about 200 meters. It is right next to the iconic Jiefangbei Monument and Chongqing Times Square.

💳 Payment Tips

Cashless payments are nearly universal in Mainland China. Before you travel, we highly recommend downloading WeChat or Alipay and linking your international credit or debit card (Mastercard, Visa, or JCB)—this will let you make seamless payments at almost every vendor in Chongqing. If you need to exchange cash at a bank counter, make sure to bring your original passport and relevant travel documents (such as a Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents). It's also a good idea to keep a small amount of cash on hand (around 300 to 500 RMB) just in case for rare emergencies.
