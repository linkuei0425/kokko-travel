Guangzhou Passes FAQ

Q: Can I use WeChat Pay or Alipay for metro and bus rides in Guangzhou?

Yes! Simply search for the 'Transport Code' (乘車碼) mini-program inside WeChat or Alipay, link your payment method, and activate the Guangzhou Metro or Bus transit code. Then, just scan to ride without buying physical tickets.

Q: Is it convenient to take the metro from Baiyun International Airport to downtown Guangzhou? How long does it take?

Extremely convenient! The Northern Extension of Metro Line 3 connects directly to Baiyun Airport T1 (Airport South Station) and T2 (Airport North Station). The train takes about 35–45 minutes directly to Tiyu Xilu Station or Zhujiang New Town (the downtown CBD).

Q: What is the difference between Guangzhou's 'Water Bus' and the 'Pearl River Night Cruise'?

The Water Bus is Guangzhou's public river transit system—it is very budget-friendly (around RMB 2–10) and ideal for daytime river commuting and sightseeing. The Pearl River Night Cruise, on the other hand, is a dedicated sightseeing tour lasting 60–90 minutes with stunning evening views and onboard amenities, priced higher (ranging from RMB 60–150+).

Q: What should I keep in mind when riding Metro Line 3?

Metro Line 3 experiences extremely heavy passenger volume. Stations like Tiyu Xilu and Zhujiang New Town get extraordinarily crowded during rush hours (07:30–09:30 and 17:30–19:30). If traveling with large luggage, it is strongly advised to avoid peak hours or take a ride-hailing car instead.
