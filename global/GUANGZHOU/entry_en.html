Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwan residents holding a valid "Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents" (commonly known as the Taiwan Compatriot Permit) can enter Guangzhou hassle-free without extra visas or endorsements. Don't have a valid permit? No problem! You can easily apply for a single-entry permit right at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport upon arrival—just make sure to bring your original Taiwan ID card, passport, a 2-inch photo, and the required fee.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Prohibited items include illegal drugs, firearms, counterfeit currency, animals, plants and their products, and illegal publications. Travelers can enjoy a duty-free allowance of up to 400 cigarettes and 1.5 liters of alcoholic beverages (with an alcohol content above 12%). If you're carrying more than RMB 20,000 in cash or foreign currency exceeding USD 5,000 equivalent, you must submit a written declaration to customs.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

[Special Information for Taiwan Passport/Taiwan Compatriot Permit Holders]: 1. Paper Arrival Card: Taiwan travelers entering Mainland China using a "Taiwan Compatriot Permit" skip the paper arrival card altogether—it's only required for foreign nationals! 2. Online Health Declaration: The mandatory online health declaration (via the mini-program) was officially discontinued on November 1, 2023. Most travelers can walk right through customs without filling out any online forms. However, if you experience respiratory symptoms like a fever or cough, please proactively declare your health condition to customs officials.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China Guangzhou Branch (Main Office): The most authoritative currency exchange location in Guangzhou, offering fair, official exchange rates. Address: No. 197 Yanjiang Middle Road, Yuexiu District, Guangzhou (Landmark: Tianzi Wharf). How to get there: Take Guangzhou Metro Line 6 to Beijing Road Station (Exit A) and walk about 8 minutes. 2. Bank of China Zhujiang Sub-branch: Conveniently located in the heart of the business district. Address: GT Land Plaza, No. 11 Zhujiang East Road, Tianhe District, Guangzhou (Landmarks: Huacheng Square, across the river from Canton Tower). How to get there: Take Metro Line 3 or Line 5 to Zhujiang New Town Station and walk straight through the underground passage directly into GT Land Plaza.

💳 Payment Tips

1. Daily spending in Guangzhou relies heavily on mobile payments. Before setting off, we recommend downloading and registering for WeChat Pay or Alipay, then linking your credit card (Mastercard/Visa/JCB) to scan QR codes and pay effortlessly. Even better, single transactions under 200 RMB are exempt from foreign processing fees! 2. The need for cash is very low here. We suggest exchanging around 500 to 1,000 RMB before your trip just to keep as emergency backup for small street vendors or rare spots that don't accept digital payments.
