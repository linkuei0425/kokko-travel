✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport Metro & Intercity Rail Transfers

Baiyun Airport is conveniently served by two stations on Metro Line 3: Airport South Station at Terminal 1 and Airport North Station at Terminal 2. Hop on Line 3 for a direct ride into the heart of Guangzhou—transfer at Jiahewanggang Station to Line 2 for Guangzhou Railway Station or Guangzhou South Railway Station, or stay on for key spots like Tiyu Xilu and Zhujiang New Town. From Airport North Station, you can also catch the Guangzhou–Qingyuan or Guangzhou–Shenzhen Intercity Railways to Guangzhou East Station, Qingyuan, Shenzhen, and beyond.

📍 Boarding at:

From T1 Arrivals, follow the 'Metro' signs to Airport South Station on Level B1. From T2 Arrivals, follow signs to Airport North Station on Level B1. Travel between T1 and T2 is completely free via metro or airport shuttle bus.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Single-ride tokens can be purchased at station ticket machines using cash, WeChat Pay, or Alipay. Alternatively, simply tap in using the Guangzhou Metro Ride Code via WeChat/Alipay or a Yang Cheng Tong card.

Recommended Airport Express & Regional Shuttle Buses

Baiyun Airport offers multiple Airport Express bus routes connecting directly to major hubs across Guangzhou (such as Tianhe Building, Guangzhou Railway Station, Haizhu District, and Panyu District) as well as neighboring cities like Foshan, Dongguan, and Zhongshan. Travel time to central Guangzhou is approximately 45 to 70 minutes.

📍 Boarding at:

Terminal 1: Bus stations are located just outside the exits of Arrival Areas A and B. Terminal 2: The bus station is situated on the west side of the terminal—follow the signs for 'Ground Transportation Center / Airport Bus'.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Tickets can be bought at the airport bus service counters inside the terminals, at self-service ticket kiosks, or online via official WeChat mini-programs ('Baiyun Airport' / 'Airport Express').

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing Services (DiDi / AutoNavi)

Both traditional metered taxis (starting fare RMB 12) and ride-hailing apps (DiDi, AutoNavi, Meituan) are quick and convenient. A ride to Tianhe District or Zhujiang New Town typically takes 40 to 50 minutes and costs around RMB 100 to 150, including highway tolls.

📍 Boarding at:

Metered Taxis: Head to the dedicated taxi ranks outside Arrival Doors A/B in T1, or outside the Arrival Hall in T2. Ride-Hailing: Follow airport signage to the designated pickup points, such as Parking Lot P3 at T1 or Parking Lot P8 at T2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Metered taxis accept Cash, WeChat Pay, or Alipay. Ride-hailing trips are seamlessly settled online within your app.
