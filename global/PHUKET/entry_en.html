Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwan passport holders enjoy visa-free entry to Thailand for stays of up to 60 days per visit (effective until further notice, with the option to apply for a 30-day extension at a local immigration office). Passports must be valid for at least 6 months upon entry. Please ensure you have a return flight ticket and sufficient cash on hand for potential spot checks (at least 10,000 THB per individual, 20,000 THB per family, or equivalent in foreign currency).

🛃 Customs Declarations

E-cigarettes, heated tobacco products, and their associated parts or liquids are strictly prohibited—violators face heavy fines or imprisonment. Duty-free allowances per person are capped at 1 liter of alcohol and 200 cigarettes (or 250 grams of tobacco). Carrying foreign currency exceeding $20,000 USD (or equivalent) must be declared to customs. Bringing protected animals, plants, related products, or illicit drugs without permission is strictly forbidden.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Great news for Taiwanese travelers arriving in Thailand by air: the requirement to complete a paper arrival card (TM6 card) is currently suspended! You simply need to present your passport and boarding pass at immigration control. While the Thai government is planning to introduce an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) online system, travelers do not need to fill out any paper arrival cards or perform online declarations before entry until that system officially goes live.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Thai Baht (THB / ฿)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. SuperRich Thailand (Central Phuket Floresta Branch): Located on the G Floor of the Central Phuket Floresta shopping mall (near the tourist information desk and supermarket). You can easily get there by taking the Airport Bus or ordering a Grab ride to the Central Phuket stop. 2. Value Plus Currency Exchange (Patong Jungceylon Branch): Located in the basement of Zone B in Jungceylon Shopping Center, Patong Beach. It is just a leisurely 5-minute walk from Patong's lively Bangla Road nightlife strip.

💳 Payment Tips

We recommend exchanging a small amount of Thai Baht (around 1,000–2,000 THB) before your trip for arrival-day airport transport and tips. For the rest of your budget, bring crisp $100 USD bills or 1,000 TWD notes to exchange at SuperRich or Value Plus in Phuket city, where rates are vastly superior to airport counters or small street kiosks. If exchanging on the street, make sure to verify they hold a valid operating license. Lastly, keep in mind that many local vendors charge an extra 3% fee for credit card payments.
