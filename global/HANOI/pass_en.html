Hanoi Passes FAQ

Q: What is the best way to get from Noi Bai Airport (HAN) to Hanoi Old Quarter?

The top choice is Express Bus 86. At just 45,000 VND one-way, it features air conditioning and luggage racks, taking you straight to Hoan Kiem Lake and the heart of the Old Quarter. If you're traveling in a group of 2–4 or carrying heavy luggage, booking a ride via Grab (approx. 250,000–350,000 VND) offers the most comfort and efficiency.

Q: Is using Grab convenient in Hanoi? Are there any scam risks?

It is incredibly convenient and widely used! Grab offers both cars (GrabCar) and motorbikes (GrabBike) throughout Hanoi. Upfront app fare calculation—whether paying by credit card or cash—completely eliminates common taxi scams like overcharging or taking unnecessary detours.

Q: Which metro lines are currently open in Hanoi, and are they useful for tourists?

Currently operating lines include Line 2A (Cát Linh Line) and Line 3 (Nhổn – Hanoi Railway Station Line elevated section). Line 2A connects central areas with the southwest, making it convenient for visiting the Temple of Literature or experiencing Hanoi's inaugural metro line. While the network is still expanding, it is a fun, handy way to explore specific spots and see modern Hanoi.

Q: Do I need Vietnamese Dong cash when riding Hanoi city buses or airport buses?

Yes! On city buses and Bus 86, conductors issue paper tickets onboard. Be sure to carry small-denomination VND cash (such as 10,000, 20,000, or 50,000 VND bills), as conductors may not be able to provide change for large notes like 500,000 VND.
