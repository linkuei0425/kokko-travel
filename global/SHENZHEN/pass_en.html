Shenzhen Passes FAQ

Q: How can travelers from Hong Kong get into downtown Shenzhen most quickly by metro?

You can clear immigration at Futian Port (connected to Metro Lines 4 and 10) or Luohu Port (connected to Metro Line 1) and step directly into the metro system. Alternatively, enter via Shenzhen Bay Port and take a shuttle bus to the nearest metro station.

Q: Which mobile payment methods are supported on the Shenzhen Metro?

The system supports the 'Shenzhentong+' WeChat mini program, Alipay 'Shenzhentong' QR code, Hong Kong's AlipayHK (Cross-border Transit Code), UnionPay QuickPass, and physical Shenzhentong cards.

Q: What is the best way to travel from Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport to downtown Futian or Luohu?

Taking Metro Line 11 (Airport Express) is highly recommended. It takes only about 30 minutes from Airport Station directly to Futian Central District, where you can easily transfer to reach Luohu or Nanshan.

Q: Is the Shenzhen 'Red Fat' Sightseeing Bus worth taking, and where can I board it?

It is ideal for first-time visitors! The open-top double-decker buses offer panoramic views, multi-language audio guides, and route stops at top sights like Window of the World, Civic Center, and Shenzhen Bay. You can easily board at popular stops such as Window of the World Station, Shopping Park Station, or Futian Railway Station.
