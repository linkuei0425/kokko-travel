Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers from Taiwan must hold a valid Mainlander Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (Taiwan Compatriot Permit). If you don't have one, don't worry! Single-entry permits (visa on arrival) can be obtained at designated ports of entry, including Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport, Luohu Port, Futian Port, and Shekou Cruise Homeport. Simply bring your original Taiwanese passport, National ID card, and a 2-inch color photo with a white background.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Contraband, politically sensitive publications, and uninspected animals, plants, or meat products are strictly prohibited. You are allowed to carry up to RMB 20,000 in cash or foreign currency equivalent to USD 5,000; any amount exceeding these limits must be declared to customs.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Great news for travelers! Taiwanese passport holders entering Shenzhen through any port (airport, land, or sea) with a valid Taiwan Compatriot Permit do not need to fill out paper arrival cards. Furthermore, China Customs has officially canceled the health declaration requirement ('black code'). No online forms are needed—simply present your Taiwan Compatriot Permit and head straight through the manual counters or automated E-channels (prior registration required) for a seamless entry!

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Renminbi (CNY / RMB)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of China Shenzhen Branch (Main Sales Department): Located at No. 2001 Jiabin Road, Luohu District, Shenzhen (near the landmark Guomao Building). Getting there: Take Shenzhen Metro Line 1 to Guomao Station, Exit B, and walk for about 3 minutes. This official branch is widely recognized for offering the fairest and best exchange rates. 2. Luohu Port Joint Inspection Building Exchange Point (Ctrip Currency Exchange): Located on the 1st floor of the Arrivals Hall at Luohu Port (near Shenzhen Railway Station and Luohu Commercial City). Getting there: Take Metro Line 1 to Luohu Station, Exit A. Ideal for travelers who need a small amount of emergency cash right after crossing the border.

💳 Payment Tips

Digital payments are nearly universal in Shenzhen! We highly recommend downloading WeChat Pay or Alipay before you leave and linking your foreign or Taiwanese credit card, allowing you to pay effortlessly anywhere in the city. If you ever need physical cash, simply use a bank card enabled for international withdrawals at a local Bank of China or ICBC ATM to withdraw RMB—the exchange rates and convenience are far superior to physical currency exchange counters.
