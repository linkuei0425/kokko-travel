✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Metro & Intercity Rail Transfers at Shenzhen Bao'an Airport

Directly connected to the basement of Terminal 3, you'll find Metro Line 11 (Airport Line) and Line 12. Line 11 is an express service taking you straight to Futian CBD in just 30 minutes, with easy transfers to Lines 1 and 5 at Qianhaiwan. Line 12 seamlessly connects Old Bao'an District with Western Nanshan. Additionally, the Guangzhou–Shenzhen Intercity Railway stops right at Shenzhen Airport Station, offering direct trains to Dongguan, Guangzhou East, and beyond.

📍 Boarding at:

Metro and Intercity Railway entrances are located on Floor B1 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC) in Terminal 3.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Scan in using the WeChat 'Shenzhen Tong+' mini-program or Alipay Transport Code, or purchase single-ride tokens at the ticket vending machines on-site.

Recommended Airport Express Buses

Shenzhen Airport operates several convenient Airport Express routes (including A1 to Futian, A2 to Longhua, A3 to Longgang, A4 to Guangming, A5 to Pingshan, and night line NA1). Travel times range from 40 to 90 minutes—perfect for reaching destinations not served by the metro or for late-night arrivals.

📍 Boarding at:

Dedicated Airport Bus waiting area on Floor 1 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC), Terminal 3.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Book in advance via the 'Shenzhen Airport' WeChat official account, or purchase tickets directly at the GTC Bus Service Counter.

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing Services (DiDi)

Metered city taxis start at RMB 13 for the first 2 km, followed by RMB 2.6 per km, with a 30% night surcharge between 23:00 and 06:00. A trip to downtown Futian takes around 35–45 minutes and costs roughly RMB 100–130. Popular ride-hailing apps like DiDi and Amap are also widely available with designated pickup areas.

📍 Boarding at:

Traditional Taxis: Dedicated taxi queue outside Floor 1 of Terminal 3. Ride-hailing: Follow the signs to the designated ride-hailing pick-up zones in GTC Parking Lots (P1/P2/P3).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay the driver via cash, WeChat Pay, or Alipay upon arrival. Ride-hailing trips are charged directly through the app.
