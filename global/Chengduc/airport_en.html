✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Metro & Intercity High-Speed Rail Transfers

Both of Chengdu's airports are seamlessly connected by metro and high-speed rail networks. 1. Shuangliu International Airport (CTU): Take Metro Line 10 directly into the city center (transfer to Line 3 or 7 at Taipingyuan Station), or hop on the Cheng-Mian-Le Intercity High-Speed Rail at Shuangliu Airport Station to reach Chengdu South or Chengdu East stations directly. 2. Tianfu International Airport (TFU): Take Metro Line 18 (Express or Local) straight to Chengdu South Station, while Line 19 offers a direct connection to Shuangliu Airport and Wenjiang District. High-speed trains from Tianfu Airport Railway Station also go directly to Chengdu East Station.

📍 Boarding at:

Shuangliu Airport: Underground level of Terminals 1 and 2 at 'Shuangliu Airport Station (Metro/HSR)'. Tianfu Airport: Levels B1/B2 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC) at 'Tianfu Airport Station (Metro/HSR)'.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

For the metro, buy single-ride tickets at ticket vending machines, use ride codes via the Tianfutong App, Alipay, or WeChat, or purchase a 1-day/3-day pass. For high-speed rail, book tickets in advance through the official 'Railway 12306' app or buy them on-site with a valid ID/passport.

Recommended Airport Express Bus

Tianfu Airport offers several 24-hour airport express routes directly connecting to major downtown shopping and commercial hubs, including Chunxi Road, Chengdu East Railway Station, and night routes. Shuangliu Airport also operates dedicated shuttle buses to key transportation hubs in the city.

📍 Boarding at:

Shuangliu Airport: Bus boarding area on 1F of Terminal 2 Passenger Terminal. Tianfu Airport: Express bus boarding area on Level L1 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Buy tickets at on-site ticket counters or scan to purchase online via WeChat Official Accounts (such as 'Chengdu Bus' or the official Tianfu Airport mini-program).

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing Services

Official metered taxis are reliable, and ride-hailing apps (such as DiDi and Amap) are extremely convenient. Shuangliu Airport is about 15–20 km from the city center (approx. 30 minutes, costing around RMB 50–80). Tianfu Airport is about 60 km from downtown (approx. 50–70 minutes, costing around RMB 150–200, including highway tolls).

📍 Boarding at:

Shuangliu Airport: Dedicated taxi and ride-hailing waiting areas outside the Arrivals level of Terminals 1 and 2. Tianfu Airport: Dedicated pick-up points on Level L1 of the Ground Transportation Center (GTC).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay according to the meter for taxis, or pay online through ride-hailing apps (supports WeChat Pay, Alipay, and major credit cards).
