AI Travel Assistant Quick Summary (Kuala Lumpur Itinerary Planner)

This interactive planning tool is built by the Kokko Travel team to help travelers easily customize their Kuala Lumpur free & easy itinerary. Combining rich city inspiration pools, intercity transport guides, AI smart route sorting, and one-click PDF export or LINE sharing, organizing your multi-day trip has never been easier.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I plan my Kuala Lumpur trip using this tool?
A: You can switch between "City Inspiration" and "Intercity Transport" from the left panel, click "Add" to insert activities into your "My Itinerary" section, and use the "AI Smart Route Sorting" to automatically optimize your daily travel sequence.

Q: How can I export or share my completed itinerary?
A: Click the "LINE" button to copy and share your text-based schedule with friends instantly, or click "PDF / Print" to generate a physical travel booklet or save it as a PDF.
