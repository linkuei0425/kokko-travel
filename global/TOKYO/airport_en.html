✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Express Trains (Narita N'EX / Keisei Skyliner & Haneda Keikyu / Tokyo Monorail)

【Narita Airport (NRT)】: 1. Keisei Skyliner: The fastest route to the city, connecting Narita to Nippori (approx. 36 mins) and Ueno. All seats are reserved. 2. JR N'EX (Narita Express): Offers supreme comfort with direct service to major hubs like Tokyo, Shinagawa, Shinjuku, and Yokohama. 【Haneda Airport (HND)】: 1. Keikyu Line: Whisks you directly to Shinagawa (approx. 13 mins), with seamless through-service on the Toei Asakusa Line to Asakusa and Oshiage. 2. Tokyo Monorail: Zips straight to Hamamatsucho (in as fast as 13 mins), where you can easily transfer to the JR Yamanote Line.

📍 Boarding at:

Narita Airport: Platforms on B1F in Terminals 1 & 2. Haneda Airport: B1F in Terminal 3 (Keikyu Line) or 2F/3F (Tokyo Monorail).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Purchase tickets at airport station counters, automated vending machines, or simply tap your IC card (Suica/PASMO). Note that express tickets and seat reservations are required for the N'EX and Skyliner.

Recommended Airport Limousine Bus & Low-Cost Express Bus (TYO-NRT)

【Limousine Bus】: Offers direct, stress-free transfers from Narita/Haneda airports to major Tokyo hotels, Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Roppongi, and Tokyo Disney Resort—saving you the hassle of lugging heavy luggage through busy train stations. 【TYO-NRT (Tokyo-Narita Bus)】: The ultimate budget-friendly choice at just 1,300 yen one-way, running frequent shuttle services between Narita Airport and Tokyo/Ginza stations.

📍 Boarding at:

Narita Airport: Bus stops located outside the 1F Arrivals Hall in all terminals. Haneda Airport: Bus terminals on 1F/2F across all terminals.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Buy on-site at Limousine Bus counters or ticket machines in the arrivals hall, or reserve your seat online ahead of time via Klook, KKday, or the official website.

Recommended Flat-Rate Airport Taxi & Ride-Hailing Apps

Enjoy peace of mind with fixed-fare taxi services connecting Narita/Haneda to various Tokyo districts (no meter-ticking anxiety in traffic, though highway tolls are separate). A 20% late-night surcharge applies between 22:00 and 05:00. Perfect for late-night arrivals, families with kids or seniors, or travelers with oversized luggage.

📍 Boarding at:

Dedicated taxi stands located on 1F of each terminal (with separate lanes for standard meter taxis and flat-rate taxis).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Walk up to the designated taxi stand directly, or book your flat-rate taxi in advance using ride-hailing apps like GO, S.RIDE, or Uber.
