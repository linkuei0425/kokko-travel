Best Value Strategy Tips

For non-subway lines (like the JR Yamanote Line or Yurikamome Line to Odaiba), simply tap your Suica/PASMO. 【4–5 Days Suburbs + City】: Use the JR Tokyo Wide Pass for the first 3 days to explore Karuizawa, Lake Kawaguchiko, or Nikko (a round-trip Shinkansen ride to Karuizawa alone covers the pass cost!). You can reload funds seamlessly without queuing for physical tickets, offering maximum flexibility.

Tokyo Passes FAQ

Q: Can I use the Tokyo Subway Ticket on the JR Yamanote Line or the Yurikamome Line to Odaiba?

No. The Tokyo Subway Ticket is valid exclusively on Tokyo Metro and Toei Subway lines. To ride JR lines (like the Yamanote or Chuo Line), the Yurikamome Line, Rinkai Line, or private railways (such as Odakyu or Keio), you will need to use an IC card (Suica/PASMO) or purchase single-ride tickets.

Q: Can I currently buy physical Suica or PASMO cards in Tokyo?

Due to global semiconductor shortages, sales of standard physical Suica and PASMO cards are currently suspended. However, foreign tourists can purchase tourist-exclusive passes like 'Welcome Suica' or 'PASMO PASSPORT' at airports and major stations (valid for 28 days, no deposit required, non-refundable). iPhone users can also add a digital Suica or PASMO directly inside Apple Wallet for free and top up using a credit card.

Q: What is the best way to get to central Tokyo from Narita and Haneda airports?

【From Narita Airport】: Take the JR N'EX (Narita Express) for Tokyo Station/Shinagawa, or the Keisei Skyliner for Ueno/Nippori (as fast as 36 minutes). 【From Haneda Airport】: Take the Keikyu Line toward Shinagawa/Asakusa, or the Tokyo Monorail toward Hamamatsucho/Tokyo Station. If traveling with heavy luggage, Airport Limousine Buses offer direct service straight to major hotels from both airports.

Q: Do I need a reservation for oversized baggage on airport express trains or the Shinkansen?

Airport express trains like the JR N'EX and Keisei Skyliner feature all-reserved seating, so your seat and luggage spot are assigned when buying your ticket. For the Tokaido, Sanyo, or Kyushu Shinkansen, if your luggage dimensions (length + width + height) total between 160 cm and 250 cm, you MUST reserve a 'Seat with an Oversized Baggage Area' in advance (free of charge during reservation); otherwise, an onboard surcharge will apply.
