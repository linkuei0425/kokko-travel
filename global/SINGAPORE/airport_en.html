✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Changi Airport MRT (SMRT CG Line) & Downtown Transfers

Changi Airport MRT Station (CG2) is conveniently located between Terminals 2 and 3. Hop on the Changi Airport Line and make an easy cross-platform transfer at Tanah Merah Station (EW4) to the East-West Line (EWL) for direct access to city hot spots like Bugis and City Hall. Alternatively, transfer at Expo Station (CG1/DT35) to the Downtown Line (DTL) to head straight to Chinatown or the Marina Bay area.

📍 Boarding at:

Located at Basement 2 (B2) between Terminals 2 and 3. If you arrive at T1 or T4, simply take the free Skytrain or terminal shuttle bus to T2 or T3. City-bound trains run from approximately 05:31 to 23:18 daily.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

No special ticket needed! You can tap in directly using a contactless credit card (Visa/Mastercard), Apple Pay, Google Pay, an EZ-Link card, or the Singapore Tourist Pass.

Recommended Public City Buses & Airport City Shuttle

Bus 36 takes you directly from the airport to key city locations like Orchard Road and Marina Bay, while buses 24, 27, 34, 53, 110, and 858 connect to various MRT transit hubs in eastern and northern Singapore. Additionally, the 24-hour City Shuttle service offers direct hotel drop-offs across downtown Singapore for around S$10 per adult.

📍 Boarding at:

Public bus bays are located on Basement 1 (B1) at Terminals 1, 2, and 3, and Level 1 at Terminal 4. The City Shuttle can be booked at the Ground Transport Concierge desks in the Arrival Halls of all terminals.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Public buses accept contactless credit cards, EZ-Link cards, or exact cash (no change given). City Shuttle tickets can be purchased using cash or credit cards at the concierge desks or self-service kiosks.

Recommended Airport Taxis & Ride-Hailing (Grab / Gojek / Zig)

Taxis operate 24/7 and run on a metered fare. Trips departing from Changi Airport incur an extra Airport Surcharge (S$6 off-peak, S$8 during peak hours daily). A 50% midnight surcharge applies between 00:00 and 06:00. You can also book rides via apps like Grab, Gojek, Tada, or Zig, with dynamic upfront pricing.

📍 Boarding at:

Designated Taxi Stands and Rideshare Pick-up Points are conveniently located outside the Arrival Halls on Level 1 at all terminals.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Taxis accept cash and credit cards. For ride-hailing services, payments are automatically processed through your linked credit card in the app.
