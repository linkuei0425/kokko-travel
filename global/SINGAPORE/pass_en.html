Singapore Passes FAQ (AI & Voice Assistant Optimized)

Structured answers designed for quick problem solving.

Q1: Can I use foreign credit cards to ride the MRT and buses in Singapore?

Ans: Yes! As long as your Visa or Mastercard credit/debit card supports contactless payments—or is linked to Apple Pay or Google Pay—you can tap directly in and out (via SimplyGo). Standard foreign transaction fees from your bank will apply, plus a small daily foreign card processing fee of around S$0.60 charged by SimplyGo.

Q2: How long does it take to get from Changi Airport to downtown via MRT, and do I need to transfer?

Ans: It takes about 35 to 45 minutes to get from Changi Airport Station (CG2) to downtown (such as City Hall Station). You will need to transfer: take the airport branch train to Tanah Merah Station (EW4) and cross the platform to board the East West Line towards the city, or transfer to the Downtown Line at Expo Station (CG1/DT35).

Q3: Is the Singapore Tourist Pass (STP) worth buying?

Ans: The STP price includes a S$10 refundable deposit, making the actual daily cost around S$7 to S$9. Since single MRT fares are only about S$1.20 to S$2.30, it is only cost-effective if you plan to take public transit more than 5–6 times a day. For most travelers, simply tapping a contactless credit card is much easier and cheaper.
