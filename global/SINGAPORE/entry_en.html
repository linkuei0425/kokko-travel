Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Travelers holding a Taiwan passport (valid for at least 6 months) can enjoy visa-free entry to Singapore for stays of up to 30 days per visit.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Singapore maintains strict customs regulations! Chewing gum, e-cigarettes, heated tobacco, smokeless tobacco, unauthorized medications, counterfeit goods, and controlled weapons are strictly prohibited. You must declare any cash exceeding SGD 20,000 (or equivalent in foreign currency). Important note: There is zero duty-free allowance for tobacco products—bringing even a single pack of cigarettes requires declaration at the Red Channel and payment of customs duties.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Say goodbye to paper forms! Travelers with a Taiwan passport do not need to complete a physical arrival card. Singapore has fully transitioned to a digital immigration system. All travelers must submit the free SG Arrival Card (SGAC) within 3 days prior to arrival (including the arrival date) via the official Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) website or the MyICA Mobile app. After completing it, you will receive a confirmation email or PDF to present alongside your passport during immigration check.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Singapore Dollar (SGD / S$)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. The Arcade at Raffles Place: Located in the heart of Singapore's financial district. Simply take the MRT Green (East West) or Red (North South) Line to Raffles Place Station (NS26/EW14) and head out Exit C. Top recommended stalls inside include 'Arcade Money Changer' and 'Sheen Express.' 2. People's Park Complex in Chinatown: Take the MRT North East or Downtown Line to Chinatown Station (NE4/DT19) and exit via Exit C. You'll find the exchange counters on the 1st-floor lobby, with 'Cragie Exchange' being a top local favorite.

💳 Payment Tips

Travelers can bring foreign cash (such as TWD) directly to these money changers to swap for SGD, as the rates are usually significantly better than those at Changi Airport counters or home banks. While credit cards and mobile payments (like GrabPay and Apple Pay) are widely accepted throughout Singapore, having SGD cash on hand is still essential when dining at hawker centres or shopping at traditional markets.
