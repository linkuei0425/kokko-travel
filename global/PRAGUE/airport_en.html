✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Bus to Metro / Train Transfer (Most Popular Budget-Friendly Route)

There are no direct metro or train lines from Prague Airport (PRG). The fastest and most convenient option is to take Trolleybus 59 (formerly Bus 119) from the airport for about 17 minutes to 'Nádraží Veleslavín' station, then transfer directly to Metro Line A (Green Line) to reach the city center (such as Malostranská, Staroměstská, or Můstek). If you are heading to the Main Railway Station, you can also take Bus 100 to 'Zličín' station and transfer to Metro Line B (Yellow Line).

📍 Boarding at:

Bus stops located directly outside the Arrivals Halls of Terminal 1 and Terminal 2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Standard PID public transit tickets apply (40 CZK for a 90-minute ticket or 120 CZK for a 24-hour pass). You can purchase and validate tickets at PID service counters in the terminals, ticket vending machines (accepting contactless payment/credit cards), or via the PID Lítačka mobile app.

Recommended Airport Express Bus (AE)

A direct express bus service to Prague Main Railway Station (Praha hl.n.) with no intermediate stops, taking around 30 to 40 minutes. It is ideal for travelers with heavy luggage, those staying near the main station, or passengers transferring to national rail trains. For late-night arrivals, Night Bus 910 offers a direct route to the city center.

📍 Boarding at:

Platform 3 at Terminal 1, and the bus stop area at Terminal 2 (clearly marked with AE signage).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

A one-way fare is 100 CZK (half-price for children). Tickets can be bought directly from the driver (cash/card), at airport tourist information centers, or used for free with the Prague Visitor Pass. Standard PID public transit tickets are not valid on this route.

Recommended Official Partner Taxi / Ride-Hailing Services (Uber & Bolt)

Uber is the official ride-hailing partner of Prague Airport. Travelers can book through the Uber app or enter their destination at dedicated kiosks inside the terminals to lock in a fixed price. The ride to the city center takes about 25–35 minutes. The Bolt app is also very popular in Prague and offers highly competitive prices.

📍 Boarding at:

Designated Uber pickup areas outside the Arrivals/Departures halls of Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 (next to the Taxi Kiosks).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Fares range from approximately 450 to 700 CZK depending on the time of day and traffic. Payment is processed entirely via credit card through the app or at the kiosks, avoiding any risk of traditional taxi overcharging.
