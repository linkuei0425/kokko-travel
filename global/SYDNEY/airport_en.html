✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Link Train (T8 Green Line)

The fastest way to get to downtown Sydney from Sydney Airport (SYD) is via the Airport Link (T8 Line). It takes just about 13 minutes from the International Terminal (T1) and around 10 minutes from the Domestic Terminals (T2/T3) to Central Station. Trains run frequently every 5 to 10 minutes. Please note that the fare includes a base train fare plus an airport Station Access Fee.

📍 Boarding at:

International Terminal (T1): After clearing customs in the Arrivals hall, follow the 'Train / Trains to City' signs down to the underground station entrance. Domestic Terminals (T2/T3): Located in the underground walkway connecting Terminals T2 and T3.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

No need to buy tickets in advance! Simply tap on with a contactless credit card, Apple Pay, or an Opal card (available for purchase or top-up at the station). A one-way fare costs around AUD 20+ (including the Station Access Fee). Pro Tip: To save money, take Bus Route 420 from outside the terminal to Mascot Station, then transfer to the T8 train to the city—this lets you bypass the airport Station Access Fee!

Recommended Public Bus (Route 420) & Private Airport Shuttles

1. Public Bus Route 420/420N: Connects the International and Domestic terminals, passing through Mascot Station and Burwood. It is the ultimate budget-friendly option if you want to transfer to a train or head to non-downtown locations. 2. Private Airport Shuttles (e.g., Ready2Go, Mozio): Offer convenient door-to-door hotel transfers, ideal for travelers with lots of luggage or larger groups.

📍 Boarding at:

Bus Route 420: Catch it at the bus stops right outside T1 International Arrivals, or at the designated bus bays outside T2/T3 Domestic Terminals. Private Shuttles: Head to the designated pickup meeting point specified in your booking confirmation.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Bus Route 420: Tap on directly using a credit card or Opal card (off-peak/short trips cost only about AUD 3–4). Private Shuttles: Must be booked and paid for online in advance via the provider's website (around AUD 20–30 per person one-way).

Recommended Taxis & Rideshares (Uber / DiDi)

The most comfortable and direct way to reach downtown Sydney, taking about 20–30 minutes depending on traffic. Taxis are metered and incur an airport departure surcharge (approx. AUD 5.50) plus road tolls. Rideshare services like Uber and DiDi are also super convenient and offer transparent, upfront pricing.

📍 Boarding at:

Taxis: Follow the 'Taxis' signs to the designated taxi ranks outside each terminal, where attendants will assist you. Rideshares (Uber/DiDi): Follow the 'Priority Pick-up' or 'Rideshare' signs. For T1 International, proceed to the express pick-up area outside; for T2/T3 Domestic, head to the designated pickup zones in the P1/P2 car parks.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Taxis: Pay on board using cash or card (note that card payments incur a ~5% surcharge). Rideshares: Charged directly to your linked card via the app (Uber/DiDi). A ride to the city center typically ranges from AUD 45 to 70.
