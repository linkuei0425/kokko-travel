Master your Sydney entry process: visa requirements, customs rules, and the best currency exchange tips at SYD Airport and downtown!

Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Most international travelers must apply for an Electronic Travel Authority (ETA, Subclass 601) or an eVisitor visa (Subclass 651) before their Sydney entry. Eligible passport holders can download the official "AustralianETA" app to apply online. The visa typically allows multiple entries with a stay of up to 90 days per visit and is valid for 12 months.

🛃 Customs Declarations

The Australian Border Force enforces extremely strict biosecurity regulations. Fresh fruits, vegetables, unapproved medicines, and certain animal/plant products are prohibited. You must declare cash equivalent to AUD 10,000 or more. Eligible travelers aged 16+ with an ePassport can expedite their Sydney entry using the automated SmartGate kiosks at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD).

📝 Incoming Passenger Card

Before landing in Sydney, cabin crew will distribute the Incoming Passenger Card (IPC). This is a mandatory legal document for your entry into Australia. It must be completed in English, signed, and presented alongside your passport at customs. Always declare items truthfully to avoid heavy fines.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Australian Dollar (AUD / A$)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. United Currency Exchange (Chinatown): Located in the heart of Sydney (Shop 7, 207 Thomas St, Haymarket). Take the L2/L3 Light Rail to 'Chinatown'.

2. Crown Currency Exchange (Town Hall): Situated in the CBD (429 George St). Take the train to 'Town Hall' and exit via the QVB. Avoid exchanging large amounts at SYD Airport due to higher fees.

💳 Payment Tips

Sydney is largely a cashless city. Contactless payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay) are universally accepted for public transport (Opal network), restaurants, and retail stores. It is highly recommended to carry only a small amount of cash for minor emergency purchases.
