Sydney Passes FAQ

Q: Do I need a physical Opal Card to use public transit in Sydney?

Not at all! You can simply tap on and off using any contactless credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) or a smart device with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Contactless payments give you the exact same fare discounts and daily/weekly caps as a physical Opal Card.

Q: Why is the Airport Link train into the city center so expensive, and how can I save money?

Sydney's International and Domestic Airport stations are privately operated, so a Station Access Fee (approx. AUD 17.34) is added to your fare. To save money, take Route 420 Bus from the airport to Mascot or Burwood Station, then transfer to a train into the city to avoid this extra fee entirely.

Q: Does Sydney public transport sell physical 1-day or 3-day travel passes?

No, Sydney has phased out physical paper 1-day and multi-day passes in favor of an automatic fare capping system. When using an Opal card or contactless payment, the system automatically stops charging once you reach the capping limits (AUD 18.70/day on weekdays, AUD 9.35/day on weekends/holidays, and AUD 50/week)—giving you the exact same benefits as a day or weekly pass.

Q: Do I need to tap on and off when riding buses in Sydney?

Yes! You must 'Tap On' when boarding and 'Tap Off' when exiting on all Sydney buses, trains, Metro, light rail, and ferries. Failing to tap off will result in the system charging you the maximum possible fare for that route.
