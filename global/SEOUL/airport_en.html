✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Incheon / Gimpo International Airport Railroad (AREX & Subway Transfers)

[Incheon Airport (ICN)] Taking the AREX Airport Railroad is the fastest way to reach the city. Choose between the Express Train (non-stop to Seoul Station, approx. 43–51 mins, all reserved seating) and the All-Stop Train (stops at every station, approx. 59–66 mins to Seoul Station, with transfers to Lines 5/9 at Gimpo Airport or Line 2/Gyeongui-Jungang Line at Hongik Univ. Station). [Gimpo Airport (GMP)] Easily reach downtown via Subway Line 5, Line 9, AREX, Gimpo Goldline, or the Seohae Line.

📍 Boarding at:

Incheon Airport T1: B1 Transportation Center Incheon Airport T2: B1 Transportation Center Gimpo Airport: Underground passageways from the International/Domestic Terminals lead directly to the subway station

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Express Train: Purchase QR-code tickets via vending machines, the official website, or online travel agencies like Klook/KKday. All-Stop Train: Simply tap your T-money card, Climate Card (valid starting from Gimpo Airport only), or buy a single-use ticket at the vending machines.

Recommended Airport Limousine Bus

Offering dedicated routes straight to major downtown hotels, this is a super comfortable option that saves you from lugging heavy bags up and down subway stairs! Popular routes include: 6001 (Myeongdong / Dongdaemun), 6002 (Hongdae / Sinchon / Dongdaemun), 6015 (Myeongdong), 6050 (Itaewon / Hannam-dong), and 6701 (City Hall / Lotte Hotel Myeongdong).

📍 Boarding at:

Incheon Airport T1: Bus stops outside Exits 4–7 and 11–13 on 1F Arrivals Hall. Incheon Airport T2: Bus Terminal at B1 Transportation Center.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Purchase tickets for specific departure times at ticket vending machines or service counters inside the terminal, or tap your T-money card when boarding (seat reservation required for certain routes).

Recommended Airport Taxi (Standard / International / Deluxe Taxi)

Perfect for late-night arrivals, travelers with excessive luggage, or family groups. Options include Standard Taxis (orange/silver/white), Deluxe Taxis (black with gold stripes, spacious with premium service), and International Taxis (yellow/black, foreign language-speaking drivers, flat zonal rates). A 20%–40% surcharge applies during late-night hours (22:00–04:00).

📍 Boarding at:

Incheon Airport T1: Designated waiting areas outside Exits 5C–8C on 1F Arrivals Hall (Standard / International / Deluxe Taxis). Incheon Airport T2: Outside Exits 3C–5C on 1F Arrivals Hall.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Line up directly at the designated taxi stands on-site. Payment is accepted via cash, credit card, or T-money. You can also book ahead using the Kakao T or Uber apps.
