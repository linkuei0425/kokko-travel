How to Find the Best Flight Deals to Seoul

Finding the best flight deals to Seoul requires knowing when to book and which airlines offer the best routes. Incheon International Airport (ICN) is the primary gateway, serving flights from all over the world.

Major Airlines Flying to Seoul

Several full-service and low-cost carriers operate direct and connecting flights to ICN. Popular options include:

  • Full-Service Carriers: Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, EVA Air, China Airlines, Delta, and United.
  • Budget Airlines (LCC): Jeju Air, Jin Air, T'way Air, and Eastar Jet (great for short-haul routes from Asia).

What is a Good Price for a Flight to Seoul?

A good deal to Seoul depends largely on your departure point. If you are flying from neighboring Asian countries (like Japan, Taiwan, or Southeast Asia), a great round-trip deal typically falls between $150 and $300 USD. For long-haul flights from North America or Europe, finding a round-trip ticket between $600 and $900 USD is considered an excellent fare.

When is the Best Time to Visit?

As mentioned in our quick facts, the cheapest months to fly to Seoul are generally from September to November (Autumn). Spring (March to May) is beautiful for cherry blossoms but can be pricier. Always try to avoid major Korean holidays like Chuseok (Korean Thanksgiving) and Seollal (Lunar New Year) to secure cheap tickets.

Getting to Downtown Seoul from ICN

Once your flight lands, the most efficient way to reach the city center is via the AREX (Airport Railroad Express), which takes about 43 minutes to Seoul Station. Airport Limousine buses and taxis are also available 24/7 right outside the arrival gates.
