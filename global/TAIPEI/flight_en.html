Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (TPE) is a major Asian hub. Premium and budget carriers including EVA Air, China Airlines, Starlux Airlines, and Cathay Pacific offer frequent direct and connecting flights from North America, Europe, and across Asia.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest month to fly to Taipei?

The cheapest months to fly to Taipei are typically September, October, and November. Booking during this shoulder season avoids the summer holiday and Lunar New Year peaks.

How far in advance should I book flights?

For the best rates, it is recommended to book your flights to Taipei 2 to 3 months in advance. If traveling during major holidays, book 4 to 6 months ahead.
