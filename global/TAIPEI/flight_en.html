Popular Routes to Taipei

Direct Flights to Taiwan from North America

If you are traveling from the US West Coast, getting to Taipei has never been easier. Searching for LAX to Taipei or SFO to Taipei? You will find daily direct flights operated by top-tier airlines such as EVA Air, China Airlines, and Starlux Airlines.

Recently, more travelers are looking up SEA to Taipei routes, as Delta and Starlux have expanded their transpacific networks. Direct flights typically take around 12 to 14 hours, ensuring you arrive in Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (TPE) refreshed and ready to explore Taipei City.

Getting from TPE Airport to Taipei City

Once you score the cheapest flights to Taiwan and land at TPE, heading into downtown Taipei is seamless. The most efficient way is the Taoyuan Airport MRT. The Express Train takes just 35 minutes to reach Taipei Main Station. Alternatively, 24-hour airport shuttle buses and metered taxis are available right outside the arrival terminals.
