✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport MRT (Taoyuan Airport TPE) / Wenhu Line MRT (Songshan Airport TSA)

【Taoyuan Airport】Hop on the Taoyuan Airport MRT Express Train (Purple Line, ~39 mins) or Commuter Train (Blue Line, ~53 mins) for a direct ride to A1 Taipei Main Station, where you can easily transfer to TRA trains, Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR), and the Taipei Metro Bannan or Tamsui-Xinyi lines. 【Songshan Airport】Located conveniently in the city center, Songshan Airport connects directly to the Taipei Metro Wenhu Line via Songshan Airport Station, making transfers across the city effortless.

📍 Boarding at:

Taoyuan Airport: Station platforms on B2 level of Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. Songshan Airport: Songshan Airport MRT Station exits located outside Terminals 1 and 2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Purchase single-journey token tickets at station vending machines, or simply tap in using an EasyCard, iPASS, select contactless credit cards, or a mobile QR code.

Recommended Airport Express Buses

【Taoyuan Airport】A variety of frequent and 24-hour express buses offer direct routes downtown. The popular Kuo-Kuang Bus Route 1819 goes straight to Taipei Main Station (~50-60 mins), while the Evergreen Bus Route 5201 serves major downtown hotels and MRT stations. 【Songshan Airport】Frequent city bus services pick up directly in front of the terminal.

📍 Boarding at:

Taoyuan Airport: Bus boarding area on B1 of Terminal 1, and 1F Lobby of Terminal 2. Songshan Airport: Bus stop shelters right outside 1F Terminal.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Purchase tickets at airport bus counters, use ticket vending machines, or simply tap your EasyCard or iPASS when boarding.

Recommended Taxis & Ride-Hailing (Uber)

【Taoyuan Airport】Queued taxis offer 24/7 service. Fares are metered with a standard 15% airport surcharge (roughly NT$1,200 - 1,500 to downtown Taipei). You can also request an Uber for pickup at designated spots. 【Songshan Airport】Located in the city center, metered taxis to downtown Taipei cost approximately NT$150 - 300.

📍 Boarding at:

Taoyuan Airport: Terminal 1, 1F Arrival Hall (outside Gate 12 on the west side); Terminal 2, 1F Arrival Hall (outside Gate 26). Songshan Airport: Taxi rank outside Gate 13.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Payment options include cash, credit card, LINE Pay, Apple Pay, or direct in-app payment via Uber.
