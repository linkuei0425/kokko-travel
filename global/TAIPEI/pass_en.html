Taipei Passes FAQ

Q: Can I tap through Taipei Metro gates using a foreign credit card?

Currently, Taipei Metro gates do not accept direct contactless payment with foreign credit cards (like Visa or Mastercard). We recommend purchasing a physical EasyCard or iPASS, or using mobile transit payments (such as EasyWallet QR codes).

Q: What is the best way to travel from Taoyuan International Airport to downtown Taipei?

The Taoyuan Airport MRT Express Train (Purple Line) is the top choice! It takes only about 39 minutes to reach Taipei Main Station for NT$150 one-way—fast, comfortable, and completely traffic-free.

Q: What are the transfer discounts available when using an EasyCard on the MRT and buses?

When using an EasyCard or iPASS, you receive an NT$8 discount per trip when transferring between the Taipei Metro and Taipei/New Taipei City buses in either direction within one hour.

Q: What are the rules and fees for returning an EasyCard and getting a balance refund?

You can request a card return and balance refund at Information Centers inside any MRT station. The NT$20 processing fee is waived if the card has been used 5 or more times AND held for at least 3 months.
