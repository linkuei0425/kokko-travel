Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwan passport holders enjoy visa-free entry when returning home. Foreign visitors are eligible for visa-free entry ranging from 14 to 90 days depending on their nationality, or can apply for an eVisa or a standard tourist visa.

🛃 Customs Declarations

Bringing animals, plants, or related products—including sausages, instant noodles containing meat, or meat-stuffed pastries—is strictly prohibited, with hefty fines up to NT$1 million for violations. You must declare any cash or valuables exceeding the equivalent of US$10,000 in foreign currency, NT$100,000, US$20,000 in gold, or RMB 20,000. Travelers aged 20 and older enjoy a duty-free allowance of 1 liter of alcohol and 200 cigarettes.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Returning Taiwanese passport holders can skip the paperwork completely—no paper arrival card or online declaration is required! Just scan your passport at the dedicated citizen lanes or automated E-Gate. Foreign visitors can easily complete the NIA Online Arrival Card on the National Immigration Agency website before boarding or upon arrival, or simply fill out a paper arrival card provided on the plane or at the airport.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

New Taiwan Dollar (TWD / NTD)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Bank of Taiwan Main Branch: Located at No. 120, Sec. 1, Chongqing South Road, Zhongzheng District, Taipei City (about a 7-minute walk from MRT Ximen Station Exit 4, near the Presidential Office Building). As the official issuing bank commissioned by Taiwan's Central Bank, it offers the best exchange rates in the country with zero transaction fees. 2. Mega International Commercial Bank (Taipei Fucheng Branch): Located at No. 91, Sec. 1, Chongqing South Road, Zhongzheng District, Taipei City (about a 5-minute walk from MRT NTU Hospital Station Exit 4, diagonally across from the Bank of Taiwan Main Branch). It offers a wide selection of foreign currencies, excellent rates, and highly efficient counter service.

💳 Payment Tips

Always remember to bring your passport (for foreign visitors) or National ID (for Taiwanese citizens) when exchanging currency at any bank in Taiwan. While the Bank of Taiwan and Mega Bank counters at Taoyuan and Songshan airports are super convenient, they charge a flat service fee of NT$100 per transaction. You can easily skip this fee by visiting the Bank of Taiwan headquarters or its downtown branches instead. Major department stores like Shin Kong Mitsukoshi and SOGO also offer exchange services, though their rates are generally less favorable than those at banks.
