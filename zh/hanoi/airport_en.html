✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Noi Bai Airport Rail & Metro Transfer Guide

Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) currently does not have direct rail access to its terminals. However, travelers can easily connect to the rail network: take Express Bus 86 to Hanoi Railway Station (Ga Hà Nội) to transfer to Vietnam Railways' North-South Line, Bus 90 to Cát Linh Station for Metro Line 2A, or Bus 07 to Cầu Giấy Station for Metro Line 3.

📍 Boarding at:

Bus waiting areas located outside the 1st Floor Arrivals Hall of both Terminal 1 (Domestic) and Terminal 2 (International).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay cash directly to the conductor on the airport bus; metro and train tickets must be purchased at station ticket windows or automated kiosks.

Recommended Express Bus 86 & Public City Buses

Express Bus 86 is the top choice for travelers, featuring modern vehicles with dedicated luggage racks. It routes through West Lake, the Old Quarter, and Hoan Kiem Lake, terminating at Hanoi Railway Station. Standard city buses are also available: Bus 07 (to Cầu Giấy), Bus 90 (to Cát Linh), and Bus 17 (to Long Biên), along with airport shuttle minibuses operated by VietJet and Vietnam Airlines.

📍 Boarding at:

Outside the 1st Floor Arrivals Hall at Terminal 1, or the designated bus lane near Pillar 2 outside the 1st Floor Arrivals Hall at Terminal 2.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay cash directly to the conductor after boarding to receive your ticket (Bus 86 costs 45,000 VND, while regular city buses cost around 8,000–10,000 VND).

Recommended Ride-Hailing Apps (Grab/Be) & Traditional Taxis

Using ride-hailing apps like Grab or Be is strongly recommended for transparent pricing and choices between cars and motorbikes. If opting for a traditional taxi, be sure to choose reputable brands such as Mai Linh (distinctive all-green cars) or G7. Alternatively, fixed-rate airport transfers can be booked at official counters inside the Arrivals Hall.

📍 Boarding at:

Dedicated taxi ranks outside the 1st Floor exits at Terminal 1 and 2, or follow the app directions to designated ride-hailing pickup points.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Grab/Be accepts app-linked credit cards or cash; traditional taxis run on meters (plus an additional airport toll of ~10,000–15,000 VND); official counter transfers can be paid by card or cash at the desk.
