Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwanese passport holders enjoy visa-free entry to Thailand, allowing stays of up to 60 days per visit. Ensure your passport is valid for at least six months from your arrival date. You should also have a return ticket and proof of sufficient funds ready—at least 10,000 THB per person or the equivalent in foreign currency.

🛃 Customs Declarations

E-cigarettes and vape liquids are strictly forbidden in Thailand—violators face severe fines or even imprisonment! Uninspected meat products, fresh fruits, and unauthorized medications are also prohibited. Duty-free allowances per person include up to 200 cigarettes (or 250g of cigars) and 1 liter of alcohol. Additionally, carrying cash over $20,000 USD or its equivalent must be declared upon arrival.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

Good news for Taiwanese passport holders arriving by air: the requirement for the TM6 paper arrival card is currently suspended, so there's no need to fill out any paper forms during your flight or at passport control! Furthermore, Thailand's planned Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) system has not yet been officially implemented. This means you don't need to complete any online arrival declarations in advance—simply proceed straight to the immigration line upon arrival.

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Thai Baht (THB / ฿)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

1. Green SuperRich Flagship Store (SuperRich Thailand): Located in the Pratunam area (Rajdamri Soi 1). Take the BTS to Chit Lom Station (E1, Exit 9), walk along Ratchadamri Road toward Pratunam for about 8–10 minutes, and turn left into Soi 1 right before the bridge—diagonally across from the CentralWorld shopping mall. 2. Orange SuperRich Chit Lom Flagship Store (SuperRich 1965): Located directly opposite the Green flagship store, also easily accessible on foot from BTS Chit Lom Station. These two flagship branches consistently offer the best physical currency exchange rates in all of Bangkok.

💳 Payment Tips

We strongly recommend bringing NT$1,000 bills directly to the SuperRich flagship stores to exchange for Baht; the exchange rate is typically better than exchanging in Taiwan beforehand or converting US dollars. If you need a small amount of Thai Baht right after landing, head to the SuperRich branch on Level B1 of Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) near the Airport Rail Link (ARL) station—it offers excellent rates nearly identical to the main downtown locations.
