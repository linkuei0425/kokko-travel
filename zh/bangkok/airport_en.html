✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Trains & Metro Connections (ARL Airport Rail Link & SRT Red Line)

[BKK Suvarnabhumi Airport]: Ride the Airport Rail Link (ARL) directly into the city center. You can switch to the MRT Blue Line at Makkasan Station (Phetchaburi Station) or connect to the BTS Light Green Line at the terminal Phaya Thai Station. [DMK Don Mueang Airport]: Walk through the skywalk connected to Terminal 2 to reach SRT Don Mueang Station. Take the SRT Dark Red Line to Bang Sue (Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal), where you can easily transfer to the MRT Blue Line to head downtown.

📍 Boarding at:

BKK: ARL station platform on Level B1 of the main terminal. DMK: Walk via the 2nd-floor skybridge walkway in Terminal 2 to SRT Don Mueang Station.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

BKK ARL: Purchase a yellow single-journey token at automated kiosks using cash/coins (15–45 THB). DMK SRT Red Line: Buy single-ride tickets at automated kiosks or tap in with a contactless credit card (Visa/Mastercard).

Recommended Airport Express Buses (Routes S1 & A1–A4)

[BKK Suvarnabhumi Airport]: Route S1 provides a direct ride to Khaosan Road and Sanam Luang. [DMK Don Mueang Airport]: 4 main airport express routes are available: A1 (to BTS Mo Chit/MRT Chatuchak), A2 (to Victory Monument), A3 (to Silom/Lumpini Park), and A4 (to Khaosan Road).

📍 Boarding at:

BKK: Bus stop outside Gate 7 on Floor 1. DMK: Outside Gate 6 on Floor 1 (Terminal 1), or outside Gate 12 on Floor 1 (Terminal 2).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay cash to the bus conductor after boarding; change and a ticket receipt will be provided. BKK S1 fare is approx. 60 THB; DMK A1–A4 fares range from 30–50 THB.

Recommended Official Queue Taxis & Ride-Hailing Apps (Grab / Bolt / InDrive)

Both airports feature official metered Public Taxis operating via automated ticket queue kiosks. Alternatively, download ride-hailing apps like Grab, Bolt, or InDrive to pre-book a ride and set your designated pickup point.

📍 Boarding at:

BKK: Get a queue ticket at the touch-screen kiosks outside Gates 4 and 7 on Floor 1. DMK: Queue outside Gate 8 on Floor 1 (Terminal 1) or Gate 14 on Floor 1 (Terminal 2). For ride-hailing apps, meet your driver at designated doors (e.g., outside Gate 4 on Floor 1 or the Departures level at BKK).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Metered Taxi Fare = Metered amount + 50 THB airport surcharge + Toll fees (approx. 25–75 THB depending on the route, paid directly by the passenger). Ride-hailing apps use system-estimated or fixed pricing (tolls charged separately).
