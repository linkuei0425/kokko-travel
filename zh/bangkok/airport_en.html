Recommended Airport Train & Subway Transfers (ARL Airport Rail Link & SRT Red Line)

[BKK Suvarnabhumi Airport]: Take the ARL Airport Rail Link directly to the city center, connect to the MRT Blue Line (Phetchaburi Station) at Makkasan Station, or transfer to the BTS Sukhumvit Line at the terminal station Phaya Thai. [DMK Don Mueang Airport]: Walk through the Terminal 2 channel to SRT Don Mueang Station, take the SRT Dark Red Line to Bang Sue (Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal), and then transfer to the MRT Blue Line into the city center.

📍 Where to board:

BKK: Terminal B1 level ARL station platform; DMK: Terminal 2, 2nd floor skybridge channel walking to SRT Don Mueang Station.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

BKK ARL: Buy yellow single-journey tokens (15-45 THB) with coins/notes at station ticket machines; DMK SRT Red Line: Buy single-journey tickets at vending machines or tap in using contactless credit cards (Visa/Mastercard).

Recommended Airport Shuttle Buses (Airport Bus S1 & A1-A4 Routes)

[BKK Suvarnabhumi Airport]: Provides S1 bus direct to Khao San Road and Sanam Luang. [DMK Don Mueang Airport]: Provides 4 main airport bus lines: A1 (to BTS Mo Chit/MRT Chatuchak), A2 (to Victory Monument), A3 (to Silom/Lumpini Park), A4 (to Khao San Road).

📍 Where to board:

BKK: 1st floor outside Gate 7 bus stop; DMK: Terminal 1, 1st floor outside Gate 6, or Terminal 2, 1st floor outside Gate 12.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Collected in cash with change/receipt by the onboard conductor after boarding. BKK S1 fare is approx. 60 THB; DMK A1-A4 fares are approx. 30-50 THB.

Recommended Queued Taxis & Ride-Hailing Apps (Grab / Bolt / InDrive)

Both major airports feature official metered taxis (Public Taxi) where you take a queue number from a kiosk. You can also download apps like Grab, Bolt, or InDrive to book rides and select designated pick-up locations.

📍 Where to board:

BKK: 1st floor outside Gates 4 and 7 at the Public Taxi touch kiosks; DMK: Terminal 1, 1st floor Gate 8 or Terminal 2, 1st floor Gate 14; for ride-hailing apps, meet at designated gates (e.g., BKK 1st floor outside Gate 4 or the Departure level).

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Metered taxi fare = Meter amount + 50 THB airport service fee + highway tolls (approx. 25-75 THB depending on the route, paid on-site by the passenger). Ride-hailing apps charge system-estimated or fixed fares (tolls extra).
