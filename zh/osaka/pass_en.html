Osaka Passes FAQ

Q: Does the Osaka Amazing Pass cover the Nankai Electric Railway or JR lines between Kansai Airport and downtown Osaka?

No. The Osaka Amazing Pass is strictly limited to designated zones within downtown Osaka. It does not cover the Nankai Express, Airport Rapid, or JR trains traveling between Kansai International Airport (KIX) and downtown Osaka.

Q: What is the fastest way to get from Kansai International Airport to Namba or Umeda?

To get to Namba, the fastest option is the Nankai Rapi:t Limited Express (approx. 38 mins). To get to Umeda/Osaka Station, the fastest route is the JR Kansai-Airport Express HARUKA direct to Osaka Station (approx. 47 mins), or a direct Airport Limousine Bus.

Q: Can I use an ICOCA card in Tokyo or other cities in Japan?

Yes! ICOCA is fully compatible with Japan's nationwide IC card network, including Tokyo's Suica and PASMO. You can use it for subways, buses, and convenience store purchases all across Japan.

Q: Can I tap my contactless credit card at Osaka Metro ticket gates?

Osaka Metro is currently rolling out contactless payment (Visa/Mastercard) testing, but IC cards like ICOCA and physical magnetic passes remain the most widely supported methods.
