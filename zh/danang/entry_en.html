Entry & Visa Requirements

🛂 Visa & Passport

Taiwan passport holders traveling to Vietnam for leisure must apply for a visa in advance. You can conveniently apply for a 90-day single or multiple-entry visa online via the official Vietnam e-Visa portal. The application fees are US$25 for single-entry and US$50 for multiple-entry, with a standard processing time of 3 to 5 business days. Once your e-Visa PDF is issued, don't forget to print out a physical copy to keep with you on your trip.

🛃 Customs Declarations

According to customs regulations, travelers carrying cash over US$5,000 (or equivalent foreign currency) or 15 million VND must declare it upon arrival. Duty-free allowances per person include: 200 cigarettes, 20 cigars, or 500 grams of tobacco; 1.5 liters of spirits (above 20% ABV) or 2.0 liters of alcoholic beverages (below 20% ABV). Please be aware that illegal drugs, unauthorized weapons/ammunition, and politically sensitive or obscene publications are strictly forbidden.

📝 Arrival Card / E-Declaration

[Entry Declaration Guidelines for Taiwan Passport Holders] 1. Paper Arrival Card: Regular tourists arriving at Da Nang International Airport (DAD) currently do not need to fill out any paper arrival cards at all. 2. Online Electronic Declaration: Vietnam currently has no mandatory nationwide online entry declaration system (the pandemic-era health declaration has been officially discontinued). Simply ensure you have your original passport (valid for at least 6 months) and your printed e-Visa ready, then line up at passport control for smooth entry!

Currency & Payments

💰 Local Currency

Vietnamese Dong (VND)

💡 Best Exchange Spots

The two most highly recommended currency exchange spots offering the best rates in Da Nang are 'Hiệu vàng Soạn Hà' and 'Hiệu Vàng Tam Thịnh'. These famous gold shops are located right across the street from the main entrance of the popular Han Market (Chợ Hàn). Getting there: Simply set your destination on Grab or take a taxi to 'Han Market'. Once there, head to the intersection of Hùng Vương and Trần Phú streets (around 116–118 Hùng Vương Street). You will easily spot these two adjacent, bustling gold shops offering fantastic rates for both USD and TWD cash.

💳 Payment Tips

1. Bring crisp, uncreased, and undamaged $100 bills for exchange, as high-denomination USD notes generally get much better rates than smaller bills or other currencies. 2. Always count your cash right at the counter and inspect the notes for severe tears or damage, as torn VND bills might be rejected by merchants. 3. Exchange counters at Da Nang Airport offer notoriously poor rates; we recommend exchanging only a small amount for immediate transportation (around $20–$50 USD) and changing the bulk of your travel funds at the gold shops near Han Market.
