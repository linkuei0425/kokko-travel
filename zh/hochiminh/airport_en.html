✈️ Ultimate Airport Transport Guide

Follow these steps for a hassle-free journey from the airport to the city center!

Recommended Airport Metro & Train Transfer Guide

Currently, Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) does not have a direct metro connection. To catch Metro Line 1, take Airport Bus No. 109 or a Grab ride to Bến Thành Metro Station in the city center. If you are transferring to the Vietnam Railways (North-South Railway), book a Grab ride to Saigon Railway Station (Ga Sài Gòn), which takes about 15–20 minutes (approx. 4 km).

📍 Boarding at:

Head to the Grab pickup area or bus stop outside the arrivals hall to start your transfer journey.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Pay cash on the bus; pay Grab via credit card in the app or with cash. Train and metro tickets should be purchased at station ticket booths or vending machines.

Recommended Airport Express & Local City Buses

Several convenient bus routes operate from the airport. Your top choice is Airport Express Bus No. 109 (bright yellow), tailored for travelers with spacious layouts and dedicated luggage racks, taking you straight to District 1's Ben Thanh Market and September 23 Park. Another option is local Bus No. 152 (green), which is the cheapest but features older buses where an extra fare may be charged for large luggage.

📍 Boarding at:

From the International Terminal, cross the first street to Line B (near Pillars 12–15). From the Domestic Terminal, wait at the bus area near Pillar 4.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Bus No. 109 tickets cost about 15,000–20,000 VND, purchasable at the platform counter or in cash on board. Bus No. 152 costs around 5,000 VND, paid in cash on the bus.

Recommended Ride-Hailing (Grab) & Traditional Taxis

Catching a ride at the airport is very popular. We strongly advise using the Grab app to ensure clear, upfront pricing. If taking a traditional taxi, stick strictly to reputable operators: Mai Linh (green) or Vinasun (white). Never accept rides from touts or unlicensed drivers to avoid overcharging.

📍 Boarding at:

For Grab, follow the app's directions to the TCP Park Garage pickup area. For traditional taxis, line up at the designated taxi stand outside the terminal.

🎫 How to buy tickets:

Grab prices are set in the app, while official taxis use meters. Please note that an extra airport exit toll of around 10,000 VND applies.
